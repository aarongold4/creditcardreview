Amex Platinum 2027 Changes: The New Hotel Credit Rules (and What to Do Before Jan. 1)

One-night Fine Hotels + Resorts stays stop counting toward the $600 credit on January 1. Airbnb Luxe is in, and business cardholders face their own changes in February.

📅 Updated November 22, 2026
✍️ By Nathan Blake
⏳ 6 min read
✓ Fact-checked

The biggest Amex Platinum change for 2027 is a new minimum stay: starting January 1, 2027, Fine Hotels + Resorts bookings must be at least two nights to qualify for the card's $600 hotel credit. Amex's own wording is: "Effective January 1, 2027, Fine Hotels + Resorts® bookings will require a minimum two-night stay to be eligible for the $600 Hotel Credit." The credit itself isn't shrinking. It is still up to $300 twice a year, and the annual fee stays at $895. Alongside that, Amex has opened the credit to prepaid Airbnb Luxe stays and added an Airbnb Experiences credit, while Business Platinum and Business Gold cardholders lose some bonus-category flexibility on February 17, 2027.

The New Hotel Credit Rule, Explained

The credit's structure isn't changing: up to $300 in statement credits for January through June and another $300 for July through December, on prepaid Fine Hotels + Resorts (FHR) or The Hotel Collection bookings made through American Express Travel with the card. The Hotel Collection already requires a two-night minimum. From 2027, FHR does too.

A one-night FHR stay in 2027 still gets the program's perks, according to The Points Guy and NerdWallet. It just won't trigger the statement credit.

Why this matters: the cheapest way to burn a $300 half-year credit has been a single night at an FHR property where the nightly rate lands near $300. That move is gone. Now you need a two-night FHR stay, and unless you find an FHR property at around $150 a night, you'll pay something out of pocket. If you already take two-night trips, nothing changes. If you've been booking a one-night staycation just to use the credit, the credit is now worth less to you, plain and simple.

Same Change on the Business Platinum

The Business Platinum Card's page carries the identical note: from January 1, 2027, FHR bookings need a two-night minimum to be eligible for its up to $600 hotel credit.

Airbnb Luxe and the $250 Experiences Credit

American Express announced on October 22, 2026 that the benefit is now the $600 Lodging Credit and covers prepaid Airbnb Luxe stays. Amex's hotel programs page uses the new name, though as of November 22 the Platinum product page still calls it the "$600 Hotel Credit." The amount and the twice-a-year timing are the same under either name. What Amex announced:

  • Airbnb Luxe counts. Prepaid Airbnb Luxe stays of at least two nights, booked through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel app, are eligible for the credit. Ordinary Airbnb listings are not. Only Luxe, Airbnb's upscale collection, qualifies.
  • An up to $250 Airbnb Experiences credit. Booking a qualifying prepaid Luxe stay through Amex Travel unlocks up to $250 in statement credits for Airbnb Experiences, once per calendar year. The credit must be used by December 31 of that year.
  • 5x on prepaid Airbnb bookings. Platinum and Business Platinum cardholders earn 5 points per dollar on eligible prepaid Airbnb bookings made through Amex Travel.

Unlike the two-night rule, the Airbnb benefits aren't tied to January 1. Amex says the Airbnb benefits began October 22, 2026. Check the benefit terms in your account before you rely on either Airbnb benefit.

The honest take: Luxe homes are expensive, so this is a perk for people who already book them. For most cardholders, it's a nice option rather than a reason to keep the card.

Business Platinum and Business Gold: February 17, 2027

Amex's business card pages flag a second set of changes, all effective February 17, 2027, and all described the same way: "select merchants only."

  • Business Platinum ($895): 2x points on U.S. shipping providers, one of the 2x key business categories (capped at $2 million of those purchases per calendar year), becomes select merchants only. So does the up to $120 Wireless Credit, which is up to $10 a month on U.S. wireless phone service bought directly from the provider.
  • Business Gold ($375): two of the six 4x categories, U.S. advertising in select media (online, TV, radio) and U.S. wireless phone service, become select merchants only. The card still pays 4x on your top two categories each billing cycle, on the first $150,000 of combined purchases per calendar year.

The card pages send you to the terms for the merchant list. If a big share of your 4x or 2x earning comes from advertising, shipping or wireless, check whether your providers make the list before February. For alternatives, see our best business credit cards.

What Isn't Changing

The issuer page lists no other 2027 changes for the personal Platinum. As of November 16, 2026, it shows:

  • $895 annual fee
  • 5x points on flights booked directly with airlines or through Amex Travel (up to $500,000 per calendar year), 5x on prepaid hotels through Amex Travel, 1x on everything else
  • $200 airline fee credit, $400 Resy credit ($100 a quarter), $300 digital entertainment credit ($25 a month), $200 Uber Cash, up to $120 per calendar year in Uber One credits, $300 lululemon credit, $219 CLEAR+ credit, a Walmart+ credit and a $200 Oura Ring credit
  • A welcome offer of as high as 175,000 Membership Rewards points after $12,000 in purchases in the first 6 months (offers vary, and you may not be eligible)

The full breakdown of each credit is in our Amex Platinum review.

What to Do Before Jan. 1

1
Use your July–December $300 hotel credit now

This is your last chance to use the credit on a one-night FHR stay. Book a prepaid FHR or Hotel Collection stay through Amex Travel with the Platinum card. If you're booking late in December, read the benefit terms on when the credit posts.

2
Plan 2027 hotel stays as two-nighters

For the January–June 2027 credit, plan around a two-night FHR, Hotel Collection or Airbnb Luxe stay you'd book anyway. A trip you'd skip without the credit isn't savings.

3
Decide whether the 2026 Experiences credit is worth chasing

A qualifying Luxe booking unlocks up to $250 for Airbnb Experiences each calendar year, and the 2026 credit must be used by December 31. Only chase it if you already planned a Luxe stay.

4
Use the other 2026 credits before they reset
  • Calendar-year credits: $200 airline fee, $219 CLEAR+, $200 Oura Ring and up to $120 for Uber One
  • Fourth-quarter credits: $100 Resy and $75 lululemon
  • Monthly credits: $25 digital entertainment, plus December's $15 Uber Cash and $20 bonus
5
Business cardholders: check the merchant list

Before February 17, 2027, look at the terms for which shipping, advertising and wireless merchants still earn the bonus, and change where you put that spending if yours aren't listed.

6
Re-run the fee math before your renewal

If a one-night FHR stay was how you got value from the hotel credit, count it at a lower value from now on. Compare against our Sapphire Reserve vs. Amex Platinum and Amex Platinum vs. Venture X breakdowns, or browse the best travel credit cards.

FAQ

Does the two-night rule apply to stays booked in 2026?

Amex says the rule takes effect January 1, 2027, so 2026 credits can still go toward one-night FHR stays until then. If you book late in the year for a stay in 2027, confirm in the terms which date counts.

Is the Platinum hotel credit still $600?

Yes. It's up to $300 in statement credits twice a year, for up to $600 per calendar year.

Is it called the Hotel Credit or the Lodging Credit?

Amex renamed it the $600 Lodging Credit in its October 22, 2026 announcement and on its hotel programs page, though the Platinum card page still says "$600 Hotel Credit." It's the same benefit either way.

Can I use the credit on any Airbnb?

No. Only prepaid Airbnb Luxe stays of two nights or more, booked through Amex Travel, qualify.

Are the Platinum's earning rates or annual fee changing in 2027?

Not according to the issuer page. The fee is $895, with 5x on eligible flights and prepaid hotels and 1x elsewhere.

Should I cancel the Platinum over this?

Not over this change alone, if you use the lounges and most of the other credits. If the one-night FHR stay was the main way you got value from the hotel credit, though, the numbers get tighter, and the Reserve or the Venture X may now be the better fit for you.

The Bottom Line

The 2027 Platinum changes come down to one real loss and two niche gains. The loss is the one-night FHR shortcut to the $300 credit, which ends December 31. The gains are Airbnb Luxe eligibility and the Experiences credit, which help people who already book high-end rentals. Use your remaining 2026 credits now, plan two-night stays for 2027, and if you carry a business card, check the merchant list before February 17.
