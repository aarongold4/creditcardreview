Editorial Policy

CreditCardReview.org is committed to providing accurate, honest, and independent credit card information. This page explains how our editorial team researches, writes, and updates our content.

Independence

Our editorial ratings, reviews, and recommendations are produced independently of our commercial relationships. No card issuer, affiliate network, or advertiser influences our ratings, written reviews, or "best" list rankings. We do not accept payment for positive coverage.

Research Process

Every card featured on our site is researched by verifying terms, rates, fees, and benefits directly against the issuer's official website. We do not rely solely on press releases or issuer-provided marketing materials. Key data points — sign-up bonuses, annual fees, rewards rates, and credit requirements — are confirmed before publication.

Accuracy & Updates

Credit card terms change frequently. We review and update our content on a regular basis to reflect current offers. When a card's terms change significantly, we update the relevant pages promptly. Each article displays an "Updated" date so you know when information was last verified.

Corrections Policy

If we publish incorrect information, we correct it as quickly as possible and note the correction where appropriate. If you spot an error, please contact us — we take accuracy seriously and appreciate reader feedback.

Affiliate Links

Some links on our site are affiliate links. These are clearly disclosed on every page where they appear. Clicking an affiliate link and being approved for a card may earn us a commission. This does not affect our editorial assessment of the card.

How We Rate Cards

Cards are evaluated on the following criteria:

  • Rewards value (40%) — Earn rates, redemption options, and real-world value of points or cash back
  • Fees and APR (25%) — Annual fee, interest rates, foreign transaction fees, and penalty APRs
  • Benefits (20%) — Travel protections, purchase coverage, lounge access, and statement credits
  • Accessibility (15%) — Credit score requirements, application process, and customer service reputation

Our star ratings reflect the card's overall score within its category. A 5-star travel card is the best in its class among travel cards — not necessarily better than a 5-star cash back card in a different category.
