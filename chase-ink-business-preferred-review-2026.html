The Ink Business Preferred is Chase's best overall business travel card. The 100,000-point welcome bonus (worth $1,000–$2,000+ in travel via partners), 3x on the most common business spending categories, and 14 transfer partners at 1:1 make it the anchor of most Chase business rewards setups. The $95 annual fee is easily justified by the welcome bonus alone in year one, and the ongoing 3x on travel and advertising keeps delivering year after year.

The 3x on "travel" on the Ink Preferred is broader than most travel cards: it includes all flights, hotels, rental cars, taxis, rideshares, trains, buses, parking, and more — not just specific portals or branded bookings. The advertising category (social media and search engine ads) is particularly valuable for businesses running Facebook, Google, Instagram, or LinkedIn campaigns.

100,000 Point Welcome Bonus — $1,000+ in Travel

100,000 Ultimate Rewards points after $8,000 in the first 3 months from account opening. At 1¢/pt cash value, that's $1,000. But via Points Boost when booking select flights/hotels through Chase Travel, or via transfer partners, these points are typically worth $1,500–$2,000+ in travel. At $95/year, the bonus alone covers 10+ years of annual fees at base redemption value.

Cell Phone Protection — $1,000/Claim

Pay your monthly cell phone bill with the Ink Business Preferred and receive coverage against theft or damage up to $1,000 per claim, up to 3 claims per year, with a $100 deductible. For businesses with multiple employee phones, this benefit can easily be worth hundreds of dollars annually. No enrollment required — coverage is automatic when you pay the bill with the card.

14 Transfer Partners at 1:1

Ultimate Rewards transfers to 14 airline and hotel partners (1:1, except World of Hyatt at 4:3). Key partners: United MileagePlus, Hyatt World of Hyatt, Southwest Rapid Rewards, Air Canada Aeroplan, British Airways Avios, Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer, Emirates Skywards, Virgin Atlantic Flying Club, Air France-KLM Flying Blue, Marriott Bonvoy, IHG One Rewards. Points Boost on Chase Travel provides up to 1.75¢/pt on select premium-cabin flights (up to 1.5¢ on other select travel) when booking through the portal.

Bottom Line

The Ink Business Preferred is the best overall Chase business card for most small businesses. The 100,000-point welcome bonus is outstanding, the 3x category coverage includes the most common business expenses, and the 14 transfer partners unlock exceptional travel redemption value. The $1,000 cell phone protection adds practical insurance value. At $95/year, this card is extremely well-priced relative to what it delivers. It's the natural anchor for a Chase business card stack alongside no-fee Ink Cash and Ink Unlimited cards.
