Southwest Companion Pass Changes for 2027: What Cardholders Should Do Before December 2

Card bonuses stop counting toward the Companion Pass in 2027. Here's what changes, the grandfathering deadline, and which card to open now.

📅 Updated November 2, 2026
✍️ By Nathan Blake
⏳ 6 min read
✓ Fact-checked

Starting January 1, 2027, Southwest credit card welcome bonuses, anniversary points and bonus points from card spending will no longer count toward the Companion Pass. Southwest announced the change on October 15, 2026. The one escape hatch: cardmembers approved on or before December 2, 2026 can still count their welcome bonus toward the pass, even if they finish the spending requirement in early 2027. If the Companion Pass is the reason you want a Southwest card, the deadline that matters is December 2.

What Changes on January 1, 2027

The Short Version

Card points still earn Rapid Rewards points you can spend on flights. They just stop helping you qualify for the Companion Pass. From 2027, the pass becomes a perk of a new top elite tier earned mostly by flying.

Today, the Companion Pass is earned with 135,000 qualifying points in a calendar year, and credit card points count. That is why a big welcome bonus has been the standard shortcut. Under the 2027 rules, these no longer count toward the pass:

Points sourceCounts toward Companion Pass in 2026?Counts from Jan 1, 2027?
Card welcome bonusYesNo (unless approved by Dec 2, 2026)
Anniversary pointsYesNo
Bonus points from card categoriesYesNo — card spending earns 1 tier qualifying point per $1
Referral bonusesYesNo
Hotel and car rental partner pointsYesNo

In place of the old threshold, Southwest is adding a Platinum elite tier, earned with 140,000 tier qualifying points or 100 qualifying one-way flights, and the unlimited Companion Pass comes with it. A lower milestone of 95,000 tier qualifying points or 75 flights earns three Companion Pass trips rather than a full pass. A-List and A-List Preferred become Silver and Gold on January 1, 2027.

That is a much higher bar for anyone who isn't a road warrior. Under the old rules, a welcome bonus plus a year of ordinary spending could get a household most of the way to 135,000. Under the new rules, $1 of card spending is worth 1 tier qualifying point, so card spending alone is not a realistic path to 140,000.

The December 2 Deadline

Southwest says cardmembers approved on or before December 2, 2026 who meet the welcome bonus requirement can count those bonus points toward the Companion Pass even if the spend is completed in early 2027. That makes the next few weeks the last window to use the classic welcome-bonus route.

Two other dates matter:

  • December 31, 2026: Southwest retires Companion Pass qualifying points at the end of 2026. Apart from a grandfathered welcome bonus, your other qualifying activity has to happen this year.
  • Through December 31, 2027: a Companion Pass earned under the old rules stays valid through the end of 2027, and it does not carry into 2028. Qualify early and you get most of two years; qualify in spring 2027 with a grandfathered bonus and you get the rest of 2027 only.

Existing Companion Pass holders keep their pass until it expires. The change affects how you earn the next one.

Which Southwest Card to Open Before December 2

Chase raised Southwest welcome offers in late October, and the higher offers run through December 2 — the same day as the grandfathering cutoff. Here is how Chase's three personal Southwest cards compare:

The Plus is the cheaper way to lock in a grandfathered bonus: 80,000 points on just $1,000 of spending gets you well over halfway to 135,000. The Premier adds 5,000 points for $50 more in fee and $1,000 more in spending, which is a fair trade only if you value its 6,000 anniversary points. The Priority's 90,000 points get you furthest, but it asks for three times the Plus's spending and more than twice the fee. For most people chasing the pass, the Plus is the smarter buy; the Priority only makes sense if you will use its seat perks and anniversary points every year.

A few cautions before applying:

  • 5/24 still applies. Southwest cards are Chase cards, so if you have opened five or more new cards from any issuer in the last 24 months (most business cards don't count), you will likely be denied. See how the Chase 5/24 rule works.
  • One personal Southwest card at a time. You can't open a personal Southwest card while you hold one, or if you earned a personal Southwest card bonus in the past 24 months.
  • Approval date is what counts. The grandfathering applies to cards approved on or before December 2, 2026. Applying on the last day leaves no margin if your application goes to review.
  • Do the math before you apply. Southwest says a grandfathered bonus that posts in early 2027 counts as if it were earned in 2026, toward the 135,000-point total. The pass that results still ends December 31, 2027, so add up your 2026 flights and card points plus the bonus and make sure you actually reach 135,000.

Is a Southwest Card Still Worth It After 2027?

For a Southwest loyalist, yes, but for a narrower reason. The free first checked bag for you and up to 8 companions on the same reservation is now the core value of a Southwest card, along with the anniversary points on the Premier and Priority. If your household checks bags on Southwest a few times a year, the Plus can still pay for itself; if you rarely check a bag, it is hard to justify after 2026.

For anyone who flies Southwest some of the time, a flexible points card now looks better. The Chase Sapphire Preferred costs $95, earns 75,000 points after $5,000 in 3 months, and transfers 1:1 to Southwest and to most of its 13 other airline and hotel partners. Without the Companion Pass shortcut, the Southwest card's main edge over a Sapphire was the pass itself. Our Southwest Plus vs. Sapphire Preferred comparison walks through that choice.

Southwest Companion Pass 2027 FAQ

Does a Companion Pass earned with a grandfathered bonus last into 2028?

No. Passes earned under the old 135,000-point rules, including with a grandfathered welcome bonus, are valid through December 31, 2027.

I already have a Companion Pass. Do I need to do anything?

No. Existing passes stay valid for their full term. The changes affect how you qualify for your next one.

Will Southwest card spending count toward Platinum in 2027?

Yes, but slowly. From January 1, 2027, Southwest cards earn 1 tier qualifying point per $1 spent, so card spending helps but won't get most people to 140,000 on its own.

What happens to A-List status?

A-List becomes Silver and A-List Preferred becomes Gold on January 1, 2027, with Platinum added above them.

The Bottom Line

If you want the Companion Pass and you're under 5/24, apply for a Southwest card early enough to be approved by December 2, 2026 — the Plus is the low-cost way to do it. After that date, the pass becomes a reward for flying Southwest a lot, not for opening a credit card, and a Southwest card becomes a bag-fee card first.
