IHG Hotels & Resorts is one of the world’s largest hotel companies, with 6,000+ properties across 19 brands including InterContinental, Kimpton, Holiday Inn, Crowne Plaza, and Hotel Indigo. The IHG One Rewards Premier Credit Card is the mid-tier entry point into the IHG loyalty ecosystem — and for $150/year, it delivers one of the most straightforward value propositions in the hotel credit card category.

The annual free night certificate is worth more than the $150 annual fee on its own at hundreds of properties. The 4th night free benefit extends that value to every award stay of four nights or more. And the 180,000-point welcome bonus — worth roughly $1,224 at 0.68¢/pt — covers the annual fee for about eight years on arrival.

Welcome Offer: 180,000 Bonus Points

New cardholders earn 180,000 bonus points after spending $3,000 on purchases in the first 3 months from account opening (limited-time offer through November 18, 2026). At IHG’s award chart, 180,000 points can cover multiple free nights at mid-tier properties or one to two nights at premium InterContinental or Kimpton properties.

At AwardWallet’s current valuation of 0.68¢ per point, 180,000 points are worth approximately $1,224 — enough to cover the $150 annual fee for about eight years. The welcome bonus alone makes a compelling case for applying, especially for anyone planning an IHG stay in the near term.

Eligibility Restriction

This offer is not available to current cardholders of any personal IHG One Rewards Credit Card, or to previous cardholders who received a new cardmember bonus on any personal IHG One Rewards Credit Card within the last 24 months. Chase’s standard 5/24 rule also applies.

The 24x breakdown at IHG hotels: 10x from the credit card, 10x base member points, plus 4x Gold Elite bonus. The 5x on flights, car rentals, dining, groceries, and gas is competitive for a $150 hotel card, and the 3x baseline on everything else is solid — nearly twice the industry standard 1x baseline.

Annual Free Night Certificate: Worth More Than the Fee

Every year on your account anniversary, you receive a free night certificate valid at any IHG property costing up to 50,000 points per night. Properties in this range include Kimpton hotels in major cities, Crowne Plaza properties, Holiday Inn Resorts, and select InterContinental locations — rooms that regularly cost $150–$300+ in cash.

If you can’t find exactly what you want within the 50,000-point cap, you can top off the certificate with additional points from your IHG account to reach higher-priced rooms. This flexibility makes the certificate usable at a much wider range of properties than the cap suggests.

Real-World Certificate Value

The Kimpton Aluna Hotel in Tulum, Mexico, costs approximately 40,000 points per night — well within the 50,000-point cap of the free night certificate. Cash rates at this property run $250–$400+ per night. Using your annual certificate here delivers 2.5–4x the card’s annual fee in a single night. NerdWallet notes the certificate alone “can more than make up for the annual fee.”

The 4th Night Free: The Best Ongoing Hotel Benefit

When you book four or more consecutive nights using IHG One Rewards points, your fourth night is free. You pay the points rate for the first three nights and zero points for the fourth. This benefit applies to an unlimited number of stays per year.

The math is straightforward: on a 4-night award stay, you get a 25% discount on your points cost. On an 8-night stay, you get the 4th and 8th nights free — a 25% discount across the entire stay. For regular IHG guests who take week-long trips, this benefit alone can save hundreds of dollars per year in points.

4th Night Free vs. The Competition

The Marriott Bonvoy Boundless does not have a 4th night free benefit. The Hilton Honors Aspire does not either. The IHG Premier’s 4th night free is a genuinely differentiated benefit that can deliver hundreds of dollars in value annually for guests who take multi-night IHG stays on points.

Automatic Gold Elite Status (Platinum at $15K spend)

Cardholders receive automatic Gold Elite status (plus 15 Elite Night Credits) for as long as they hold the card; spending $15,000 in a calendar year unlocks Platinum Elite — no minimum nights required. Platinum Elite is the third tier in the IHG program (out of five) and includes:

  • Guaranteed room availability when booking at least 72 hours in advance
  • Complimentary room upgrades (based on availability)
  • Welcome amenity at check-in
  • Late checkout (based on availability)
  • 60% bonus points on qualifying paid stays
  • Early check-in when available

Platinum Elite is a meaningful status level. At most hotel programs, this tier requires 50–75 qualifying nights per year. Earning it via $15,000 in card spend is a significant shortcut for moderate IHG travelers.

Other Noteworthy Benefits

Global Entry / TSA PreCheck Credit

Up to $120 in statement credits every four years for Global Entry, TSA PreCheck, or NEXUS application fees. For a $150 annual fee card, this is an uncommon perk — most hotel cards at this price point don’t include it.

$50 United TravelBank Credit

After registering your IHG Premier with your United MileagePlus account, you receive up to $50 in United TravelBank Cash each calendar year. This directly offsets the annual fee, effectively bringing the net cost down to $100 for United flyers.

$15,000 Spend Milestone

If you spend $15,000 on the card in a calendar year, you receive Platinum Elite status, 5 Elite Night Credits, and 15,000 bonus points. Since the October 1, 2026 refresh, the card also includes up to $100 a year in food and beverage credits ($25 per quarter) and a $100 airline credit after $250+ in direct airline spend.

20% Discount on Points Purchases

When you buy IHG points to top off an award stay, you save 20% on the purchase price. Useful if you’re close to a free night and don’t want to wait to earn more points organically.

Who Should Get the IHG One Rewards Premier?

Get it if you:

  • Stay at IHG properties at least once or twice per year and can use the annual free night certificate
  • Take multi-night IHG award stays where the 4th night free delivers substantial points savings
  • Want automatic Gold Elite status and a spend path to Platinum
  • Are a United Airlines flyer who can use the $50 TravelBank credit to further offset the fee

Skip it if you:

  • Stay across multiple hotel brands — a flexible travel card like the Chase Sapphire Preferred transfers to IHG but also to Hyatt, Marriott, and airlines
  • Rarely stay at IHG properties — the annual free night and status benefits require IHG stays to deliver value
  • Already hold 5+ new credit cards in the past 24 months (Chase 5/24)

The Bottom Line

The IHG One Rewards Premier is an easy card to recommend for regular IHG guests. The annual free night certificate consistently delivers more value than the $150 annual fee — making this one of the clearest “pays for itself” hotel cards on the market. Stack on the welcome bonus, 4th night free on award stays, automatic Gold Elite status, and Global Entry credit, and the value case is overwhelming for anyone who chooses IHG properties with any regularity.

The limitation is the same as every co-branded hotel card: if you’re not loyal to IHG, the card’s value collapses. IHG points are also worth less per point than Hyatt or Chase Ultimate Rewards. For brand-agnostic travelers, a transferable points card like the Chase Sapphire Preferred — which transfers to IHG at 1:1 — is the more versatile choice.

But for IHG loyalists, this card is a no-brainer. The math works at any level of engagement with the IHG network.
