The Gap Encore Mastercard launched February 24, 2026 as a complete overhaul of Gap Inc.'s credit card and loyalty program. It replaced the old Gap Good Rewards Mastercard — and the upgrade is significant. The most distinctive feature: 3x points at all eligible apparel retailers, not just Gap family brands. That means H&M, Zara, Madewell, J.Crew, Nordstrom, TJ Maxx, and virtually any clothing store earns 3x. No other $0-annual-fee store card rewards you broadly across the entire apparel category like this.

The Encore Market — What Makes It Unique

Points redeem at 500 pts = $1 (0.2 cents per point, so 25 pts/$1 ≈ 5% back) toward two options: purchases at Gap Inc. brands, or the Encore Market — a curated online marketplace exclusive to Encore cardmembers featuring one-of-a-kind experiences, limited products, exclusive collaborations, and access to Gap brand events. The Encore Market is genuinely unusual for a store card; it's Gap's attempt to create a loyalty ecosystem beyond just discount coupons.

All-Access Status — Automatic for Cardmembers

Cardmembers are automatically enrolled at All-Access tier — the highest level of the Encore loyalty program. All-Access benefits include:

  • Free standard shipping on orders of $35 or more across all Gap Inc. brands
  • Pick Your Own Sale Day: Once per year, choose a day to receive 20% off with your card across Gap Inc. brands
  • First in line for select new collections and product drops
  • Extended returns: Up to 60 days to return (30 days receive merchandise credit)
  • Birthday bonus: Extra rewards or discount around your birthday
  • 25 pts per $1 on physical gift cards purchased in-store at Gap brands

3x on All Apparel Is Genuinely Unusual

Most store-branded cards earn points only at their own brand. The Gap Encore's 3x at all eligible apparel retailers — coded by merchant category — turns this into a functional fashion cash back card. A shopper spending $200/month across various clothing stores earns the equivalent of $6 back in Gap rewards monthly. The main limitation: those rewards only redeem at Gap Inc. brands or the Encore Market, so they're not cash.

33.24% APR — Same High-Rate Warning as All Store Cards

The Gap Encore Mastercard carries a 33.24% variable APR — the standard high rate for retail co-branded cards. Do not carry a balance on this card. The math is simple: a $500 balance carried for one year costs ~$166 in interest at this rate, far exceeding any points earned. This card only makes sense as a pay-in-full-every-month card.

Pros & Cons

What We Like

  • 3x at all eligible apparel retailers — unique in the store card category
  • 5x at Gap, Old Navy, Banana Republic, and Athleta — four major brands
  • Automatic All-Access status with free shipping, extended returns, and sale day
  • Encore Market offers genuine exclusive access beyond just discounts
  • 20% off first purchase within 14 days of account opening
  • No annual fee — zero cost to keep the card for ongoing Gap brand shopping
  • Issued by Barclays — generally well-regarded issuer vs. some store card issuers

What to Watch Out For

  • 33.24% APR — dangerous if you carry a balance
  • Rewards only redeem at Gap Inc. brands or Encore Market — no cash flexibility
  • 3% foreign transaction fee — avoid using internationally
  • Welcome discount is 20% — smaller than American Eagle's 30%
  • No bonus outside apparel/Gap brands — groceries, gas, dining earn just 1x

Bottom Line

The Gap Encore Mastercard is the best store card for fashion shoppers — not just Gap loyalists. The 3x at all eligible apparel retailers is genuinely unusual and makes this card worth using every time you buy clothes anywhere. Pair it with a flat-rate cash back card for non-apparel spending, and you have an efficient two-card clothing wallet. The 33.24% APR is the one dealbreaker — treat this as a pay-in-full card only, and it delivers strong value at zero annual cost.
