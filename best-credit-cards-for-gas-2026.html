1. Costco Anywhere Visa® Card by Citi — Best for Costco Gas

The Costco Anywhere Visa earns 4% cash back on eligible gas and EV charging purchases worldwide (up to $7,000 per year, then 1%) — the highest gas rate available on a widely available credit card. Costco gas stations typically price 10–30 cents per gallon below the surrounding market, so Costco members using this card at Costco pumps capture both the price savings and 5% cash back (4% at other gas stations). The card charges no annual fee beyond your required Costco membership ($65/yr). It also earns 3% on restaurants and travel, 2% at Costco and Costco.com, and 1% elsewhere.

2. Bank of America® Customized Cash Rewards — Best 0% Fee Gas Card (Year 1)

The BofA Customized Cash Rewards earns 6% in your choice category in year one — and gas is one of the six choices. At 6% on $300/month in gas = $216/year back, no other no-fee card comes close. After year one, the rate drops to 3% on gas (still among the best no-fee gas rates). The combined cap of $2,500/quarter for your choice category plus grocery/wholesale club spending is the main constraint for drivers who also shop heavily at supermarkets.

3. Sam's Club® Mastercard® — Best for Sam's Club Members

The Sam's Club Mastercard earns 5% cash back on gas anywhere (not just Sam's Club) on up to $6,000 per year — making it one of the strongest gas cards for Sam's Club members. It also earns 3% on dining and takeout, 1% on Sam's Club purchases (5% for Plus members), and 1% everywhere else. There's no annual fee beyond Sam's Club membership. Like the Costco card, it combines membership warehouse savings with a top-tier gas reward.

How to Choose the Best Gas Credit Card

The right gas card depends on one key question: do you shop at a warehouse club? If yes, the Costco Anywhere Visa (4% gas, $65/yr membership) or Sam's Club Mastercard (5% gas, $60/yr membership) will earn more than any standalone gas card — assuming you already have or would get a membership for other reasons. If no, the BofA Customized Cash Rewards' 6% in year one (then 3%) is the strongest no-fee option, or the Citi Strata Premier if you want unlimited 3x gas plus travel and grocery earning with no cap.

One strategic note: pair a dedicated gas card with a flat-rate 2% card. Use the gas card at the pump, the flat-rate card everywhere else. This two-card system captures elevated gas earnings without giving up rewards on non-gas spending where the gas card earns only 1%.
