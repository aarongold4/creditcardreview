The Costco Anywhere Visa Business Card by Citi is the small-business counterpart to the personal Costco Anywhere Visa — and the two cards are nearly identical in their reward structure. The same 5% on Costco gas, 4% on other gas and EV charging, 3% on dining and travel, 2% at Costco and Costco.com, and 1% elsewhere. The key differences are practical: the business card requires a Costco Business membership (rather than personal Gold Star), rewards over $10,000 are distributed differently, and the card reports primarily to Experian on your personal credit file rather than to business credit bureaus.

Who Should Get the Business Version?

The business card makes most sense for:

  • Small business owners with Costco Business memberships — contractors, restaurants, retailers, and other businesses that buy supplies in bulk through Costco Business Centers
  • Businesses with high fuel spending — fleets, delivery services, contractors, and transportation businesses that fill up regularly and want to maximize the 4–5% gas rate on business purchases
  • Business owners who dine out with clients frequently — the unlimited 3% on restaurants is a strong category for client entertainment spending
  • Those who want to keep business and personal expenses separated — even if the earn rates are the same as the personal card, using a dedicated business card simplifies bookkeeping and expense reporting

Costco Business Centers: A Key Advantage

Costco Business Centers are separate warehouse locations stocked with commercial quantities of food service, janitorial, and office supplies unavailable at standard Costco warehouses. Business cardholders can shop these locations and earn 2% back on all purchases — the same as at regular Costco warehouses. For restaurant owners, caterers, or any food-service business, Costco Business Centers offer commercial pricing with credit card rewards stacked on top.

Annual Certificate — Same Structure as Personal Card

Rewards accumulate all year and are issued as an Annual Credit Card Reward Certificate after the February billing statement closes. The certificate is redeemable at any U.S. Costco warehouse for cash or merchandise in a single transaction, valid through December 31 of the issuance year. For businesses earning over $10,000 in annual cash back (very high-volume Costco spenders), the distribution process differs — contact Citi for details on large-balance redemption options.

Reports to Personal Credit, Not Business Credit

The Costco Anywhere Visa Business Card reports account activity to Experian on your personal credit file — not to Dun & Bradstreet or other business credit bureaus. This means: (1) your business card balance affects your personal credit utilization, and (2) the card won't help you build business credit history. If building a separate business credit profile is a priority, consider a card that reports to commercial bureaus like the Ink Business Cash from Chase or Amex Business Gold.

Pros & Cons

What We Love

  • Identical earn rates to the personal Costco Visa — excellent gas and travel rates
  • No annual card fee — just the Costco Business membership cost
  • No foreign transaction fee — use for international business travel and dining
  • Employee cards available at no extra cost
  • Access to Costco Business Centers for commercial bulk purchasing
  • 3% unlimited on dining — strong for client entertainment expenses

What to Watch Out For

  • No welcome bonus — weak first-year value vs. most small business cards
  • Annual certificate redemption only — no access to rewards until February
  • Reports to personal credit, not business credit bureaus
  • No balance transfer option (available on personal Costco card)
  • Excellent credit required (720+) — high bar for business approval
  • 2% at Costco is modest for an in-club rate — business owners buying large volumes may find cards with higher store rates more valuable

Bottom Line

The Costco Anywhere Visa Business Card is the right card for small business owners who already have a Costco Business membership, spend heavily on gas and client dining, and are comfortable with the annual certificate redemption cycle. Its earn rates are genuinely excellent for a no-annual-fee business card — particularly the unlimited 3% on dining and travel, which rivals business cards with $95+ annual fees. The primary limitations are the no-welcome-bonus structure, annual-only rewards access, and personal credit reporting. For Costco Business shoppers who can live with these trade-offs, it's one of the best no-fee business cash back cards available.
