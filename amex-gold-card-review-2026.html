📅 Updated April 30, 2026 — Card Refresh

American Express refreshed the Gold Card on April 30, 2026. New additions: 5x points on prepaid hotels booked via AmexTravel.com (up from 2x); complimentary Hertz Five Star status (enrollment required); updated dining credit partners (now includes Buffalo Wild Wings and Wonder). Annual fee unchanged at $325. Welcome offer spend requirement raised to $8,000 in 6 months. All terms on this page reflect the refreshed card.

The American Express Gold Card is the best credit card available for people who spend heavily on dining and groceries. Its 4x earning rate on restaurants worldwide and U.S. supermarkets is unmatched at any annual fee, and the $120 dining credit plus $120 Uber Cash credit effectively reduce the net annual fee to $85 for anyone who uses them fully.

This is primarily a rewards-earning card, not a travel-perks card. There's no lounge access, no TSA PreCheck credit, and only basic travel insurance (trip delay and baggage). But for the right spender — someone whose biggest categories are food and dining — it earns points at a rate no other card can touch.

Welcome Offer: Up to 100,000 Points

New cardholders can earn up to 100,000 Membership Rewards points after spending $8,000 on eligible purchases in the first 6 months. Welcome offers vary — Amex shows your exact offer at application with no credit score impact until you accept.

At a conservative valuation of 1.5¢ per Membership Rewards point (transferable to airlines and hotels), 100,000 points are worth $1,500 in travel. That covers the $325 annual fee for over four years.

Once-Per-Lifetime Rule

Amex enforces a strict once-per-lifetime welcome bonus policy on this card. If you have or have had the Amex Gold Card, you may not be eligible for the welcome offer again — and under Amex's "family" rule, current or past Platinum Card holders may also be ineligible for the Gold bonus. If you want both, apply for the Gold first. Amex will show a notice before you accept if you're not eligible.

The $25,000 Supermarket Cap

The 4x rate at U.S. supermarkets applies to the first $25,000 in purchases per calendar year, then drops to 1x. For most households this cap is never an issue — $25,000 in groceries equals about $2,083/month, well above average household grocery spending.

Transfer Partners: 20+ Programs

Membership Rewards points transfer to 20+ airline and hotel partners, including Delta SkyMiles, British Airways Avios, Air France/KLM Flying Blue, ANA Mileage Club, Marriott Bonvoy, and Hilton Honors. Most transfers are at 1:1 ratios. This is what gives Amex Gold points their real value — a single 4x dining purchase becomes 4 airline miles that can be worth 8¢+ when redeemed for business class.

The Gold is for the spender who eats out and cooks at home. The Platinum is for the frequent traveler who values lounges and hotel status. They're complementary, not competitive — many people hold both.

The Bottom Line

The Amex Gold Card is the best card available if dining and groceries are your two biggest spending categories. The 4x rate on both is genuinely unmatched — no other card, at any fee, earns as much on food. The $424+ in annual credits reduce the net cost substantially, and 20+ transfer partners give your points real travel value.

It's not a complete travel card — for lounge access or trip protections, pair it with a Chase Sapphire or Amex Platinum. But for pure points accumulation on everyday spending, there's nothing better.
