The Delta SkyMiles® Gold American Express Card is the sweet spot in Delta’s personal card lineup — sitting between the no-fee Blue and the $350 Platinum. It’s the card for Delta flyers who want meaningful travel benefits without paying premium-card prices. The free checked bag alone can cover the annual fee in a single round trip. The TakeOff 15 discount on award flights makes your SkyMiles go further every time you redeem them.

One important note: the card charges $0 for the first year, then $150 starting at your first renewal. That first year is a genuine free trial — you get all the benefits and the full welcome offer without paying anything upfront.

Welcome Offer: Up to 80,000 Bonus Miles

New cardholders can earn up to 80,000 bonus miles after spending $2,000 on eligible purchases in the first 6 months. Welcome offers vary and you may not be eligible — Amex shows you your personalized offer after applying, with no impact to your credit score until you accept.

At TPG’s April 2026 valuation of 1.2¢ per SkyMile, 80,000 miles are worth up to $960 in Delta flights. That covers the $150 annual fee for over six years. The $2,000 spend requirement over 6 months ($334/month) is very achievable for most cardholders.

Once Per Lifetime Rule

Amex enforces a strict once-per-lifetime welcome bonus policy. If you’ve previously received a welcome offer on any Delta SkyMiles Amex card, you are not eligible for the welcome offer again. Additionally, Amex’s “family language” policy means having a higher-tier Delta card may affect your eligibility for lower-tier card bonuses. Check your eligibility before applying.

Free Checked Bag: The Fee Justifier

Cardholders receive their first checked bag free on Delta flights — even on Basic Economy tickets. This benefit extends to up to 8 companions on the same reservation, making it extraordinarily valuable for families and groups.

TakeOff 15: 15% Off Every Award Flight

Every time you use SkyMiles to book a Delta-operated award flight, the Gold card gives you 15% off the miles required. This applies to all Delta award bookings through delta.com or the Fly Delta app. It does not apply to partner-operated flights or to taxes and fees.

This benefit is permanent — it applies to every award redemption for as long as you hold the card. At current mile valuations, it effectively adds ~0.18¢ per mile to every redemption. On an 80,000-mile business class redemption, TakeOff 15 saves you 12,000 miles — worth about $144 at 1.2¢/mile. Over years of Delta flying, this compounds into substantial savings.

TakeOff 15 Compounds Over Time

If you redeem 50,000 SkyMiles per year for Delta flights, TakeOff 15 saves you 7,500 miles annually — worth ~$90 at 1.2¢/mile. Over 5 years that’s $450 in savings from this single benefit alone, on top of everything else the card provides.

Other Key Benefits

Priority Boarding

Gold cardholders board in Zone 5 on all Delta flights — ahead of the general boarding group but after Delta One, First Class, and Medallion members. On full flights this makes a real difference for overhead bin space, especially without checked bags.

$200 Delta Flight Credit

Spend $10,000 on the card in a calendar year and receive a $200 Delta flight credit toward future Delta travel. At that spending level ($833/month), this effectively makes the card free and then some — the $200 credit exceeds the $150 annual fee.

$100 Delta Stays Credit

Receive up to $100 back per year as a statement credit when booking prepaid hotels or vacation rentals through Delta Stays on delta.com. If you book any Delta vacation package, this credit alone nearly covers two-thirds of the annual fee.

20% Back on Inflight Purchases

Use the card for eligible inflight purchases (food, beverages, headsets — not Wi-Fi) and receive a 20% statement credit back on those charges. A small but consistent perk for frequent flyers.

No Foreign Transaction Fees

No surcharge on international purchases — useful since Delta flies to 300+ destinations in 50+ countries and cardholders often spend abroad.

Travel Protections

  • Baggage insurance — checked bags up to $500, carry-on up to $1,250
  • Car rental loss and damage coverage — secondary coverage when you decline CDW
  • Purchase protection — 90 days against theft or damage, up to $1,000 per claim
  • Extended warranty — up to 1 additional year on eligible warranties of 5 years or less
  • Global Assist Hotline — 24/7 emergency assistance when traveling 100+ miles from home

Is the $150 Annual Fee Worth It?

The $150 fee pays for itself through the free checked bag benefit for any cardholder who flies Delta and checks a bag at least twice per year. Add TakeOff 15, priority boarding, and the $100 Delta Stays credit, and the card delivers significantly more than $150 in value for regular Delta passengers.

The card is not worth it if you rarely fly Delta, fly without checked bags, or prefer flexible rewards over airline-specific miles. In those cases, a card like the Chase Sapphire Preferred — which transfers points to Delta at 1:1 — delivers better overall value with no airline lock-in.

Who Should Get the Delta SkyMiles Gold?

Get it if you:

  • Fly Delta at least 2–3 times per year and check a bag — the bag savings cover the fee
  • Want to earn SkyMiles on everyday dining and grocery spending
  • Regularly book Delta award flights and want the permanent 15% discount on miles required
  • Are starting out with Delta and want a year to try the card risk-free ($0 first year)

Skip it if you:

  • Fly multiple airlines — a flexible card like the Chase Sapphire Preferred transfers to Delta and 13 other programs
  • Want lounge access — look at the Delta SkyMiles Reserve ($650/yr)
  • Already have the Delta Platinum or Reserve and the welcome offer exclusions apply
  • Fly Basic Economy and never check bags — the main perk becomes less valuable

The Bottom Line

The Delta SkyMiles Gold is the most practical Delta credit card for the majority of Delta flyers. The free checked bag pays for itself quickly, TakeOff 15 delivers lasting value on every award redemption, and the $0 first year makes it genuinely risk-free to try. At $150/year from year two, it requires 2–3 bag-fee savings annually to break even — an easy bar for anyone who flies Delta with any regularity.

It’s not a card for everyone. Delta loyalty is the prerequisite. But for the tens of millions of Americans who fly Delta regularly, the Gold is the most cost-efficient way to extract meaningful value from their SkyMiles membership.
