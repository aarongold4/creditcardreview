The Amex Blue Cash Everyday is the best no-annual-fee card for households that spend meaningfully on groceries, gas, and online shopping — without hitting the $500/month grocery mark where the Blue Cash Preferred's 6% rate starts earning more net of its $95 fee. Three bonus categories covering the majority of most household budgets, all at 3%, with the longest 0% APR intro period in the no-fee cash back class (15 months), and zero annual cost to carry.

What Counts as a "U.S. Supermarket"?

Amex defines U.S. supermarkets as stores primarily selling food and household products, identified by their merchant category code. Traditional grocery chains earn 3%: Kroger, Publix, Safeway, HEB, Whole Foods, Trader Joe's, Albertsons, Stop & Shop, and most regional chains. Superstores and warehouse clubs do not qualify — Walmart, Target, Costco, and Sam's Club all earn only 1% in store. Shop at a traditional grocery store, not a supercenter, to capture the 3%.

Online Retail: Broader Than You'd Expect

The 3% online retail category applies to purchases made online with U.S. merchants — not categorized by retailer type, but by purchase channel. Amazon.com, BestBuy.com, Target.com, Walmart.com, Chewy, Home Depot online, Macy's online, and similar all typically earn 3%. Purchases at those same merchants made in-person earn only 1%. This makes the card particularly strong for online shoppers even at non-grocery retailers.

The $6,000 Per-Category Cap

Each bonus category is capped at $6,000 in spending per calendar year — exactly $500/month. The average U.S. household spends roughly $500–$700/month on groceries. If you're a family of 4 or more, you'll likely hit the grocery cap mid-year and earn just 1% on groceries after that. Once capped, consider the Blue Cash Preferred (6% on groceries for $95/year) or the Capital One Savor (3% on groceries at non-superstores, no cap, no annual fee).

Extra Benefits

  • Disney Bundle credit: Up to $7/month back on an eligible Disney+ bundle subscription (enrollment required) — $84/year in value
  • Amex Offers: Rotating bonus deals in your account (10% off specific merchants, statement credits, etc.) that stack on top of base cash back
  • 0% intro APR for 15 months on purchases and balance transfers — longest available on a no-fee cash back card
  • Purchase protection: 90 days against damage or theft, up to $1,000 per item
  • Car rental loss and damage insurance when you pay with the card and decline CDW
  • Global Assist Hotline: 24/7 emergency travel assistance when traveling more than 100 miles from home

2.7% Foreign Transaction Fee — Keep This Card Domestic

The Blue Cash Everyday charges 2.7% on international purchases — effectively wiping out the 3% earn rate on any foreign transaction. Additionally, Amex acceptance is lower internationally than Visa or Mastercard. This card is best reserved for U.S. grocery, gas, and online retail spending. Pair it with a no-fee travel card (Capital One Venture, Chase Sapphire Preferred) for international use.

If you spend more than $3,167/year (~$264/month) on groceries, the Preferred earns enough extra at 6% to cover its $95 fee. Below that, the Everyday earns more net. For gas and transit, both cards earn 3%. For online retail, the Everyday actually wins (3% vs. 1%). The Preferred's streaming category (6%) adds value if you pay for Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, and similar. Most families with multiple streaming services and heavy grocery bills favor the Preferred; single adults and smaller households often do better with the Everyday.

Bottom Line

The Blue Cash Everyday is the best no-annual-fee option for households spending under $500/month on groceries who also want 3% on gas and online retail. The 15-month 0% APR is unbeatable in its class. The Disney Bundle credit and Amex Offers add real ongoing value beyond the base earn rate. Its weaknesses — the per-category spending cap and foreign transaction fee — are manageable with a basic card pairing strategy: use the BCE for domestic grocery/gas/online spending, use a no-fee travel card for everything else.
