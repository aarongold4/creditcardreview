Best Airline Credit Cards of 2026: Free Bags, Miles & Status for Every Flyer

The right airline card saves you $60–$120 per round trip on checked bags alone — before counting miles, priority boarding, and lounge access. We reviewed every major airline card to find the best for Delta, United, Southwest, and American Airlines.

⏱ 12 min read

Airline credit cards offer benefits that general travel cards can't match: free checked bags on every flight, priority boarding, companion certificates, and miles that accumulate toward elite status. For someone who flies a preferred carrier even three or four times per year, a co-branded airline card frequently pays for itself on bag fees alone.

The key is loyalty. Airline cards only make sense if you consistently fly one carrier. If you fly different airlines depending on price and schedule, a flexible rewards card like the Chase Sapphire Preferred gives far more value — its points transfer to 10 airline programs at 1:1.

Co-Branded Airline Card vs. Flexible Rewards Card: Which Is Right for You?

Choose a co-branded airline card if...

  • You fly one airline at least 4–6 times per year
  • You check bags — the free bag perk pays for most co-branded cards in a single round trip
  • You're working toward that airline's elite status
  • Your home airport is a hub for that specific carrier

Choose a flexible card (Chase Sapphire Preferred) if...

  • You fly different carriers depending on price and route
  • You want points that work on multiple airlines AND hotel chains
  • You don't check bags often and won't use carrier-specific perks
  • You're new to travel rewards and want maximum flexibility to learn

The Companion Pass — Southwest's Secret Weapon

The Southwest Companion Pass allows one designated person to fly free (only paying taxes and fees) on every Southwest flight you take for the remainder of the calendar year you earn it plus the full following year. Earn 135,000 qualifying points in a calendar year to unlock it. Through 2026, the Priority card's 90,000-point sign-up bonus plus regular spending can get you most of the way there (from January 1, 2027, Southwest says card welcome-bonus, spending and anniversary points will no longer count toward the pass) — and it's the single most valuable benefit available from any airline loyalty program.

The Bottom Line

For Delta flyers, the Delta SkyMiles Gold is the clear starting point — $0 first year, free bags for the whole family, and a welcome offer that can cover several round trips. The same logic applies across the board: the United Explorer, Southwest Priority, and AAdvantage Platinum Select each justify their modest annual fees on bag savings alone for their respective carriers.

If you don't have a preferred airline, skip the co-branded cards entirely. The Chase Sapphire Preferred gives you 75,000 points that transfer to 10 airlines — far more flexibility than any single co-branded card at the same $95 annual fee.
