The BankAmericard has one job: giving you the longest 0% APR window available on a credit card so you can pay off debt or finance a large purchase without interest. At 21 billing cycles of 0% on both purchases and balance transfers, it's among the longest intro periods in the market. It earns no rewards, has no welcome bonus, and offers no ongoing value after the intro period ends — but if interest-free financing is what you need, it delivers that more effectively than nearly any other card.

0% APR: 21 Billing Cycles — How It Works

The BankAmericard offers 0% intro APR for 21 billing cycles on:

  • New purchases — any purchase made from account opening through the end of the 21-billing-cycle period accrues no interest
  • Balance transfers — balances moved from other credit cards within the first 60 days of account opening also carry 0% APR for 21 billing cycles

After 21 billing cycles, the variable APR of 14.99%–25.99% applies to any remaining balance. The exact rate within that range depends on your creditworthiness at application. 21 billing cycles is approximately 21 months — significantly longer than the market average of 11–13 months for 0% intro APR cards.

The Balance Transfer Fee: 5%

Moving a balance to the BankAmericard costs 5% of the transferred amount (minimum $10). On a $5,000 balance transfer, that's a $250 upfront fee. Compare this to the interest you'd pay on a high-rate card over the same period: a $5,000 balance at 20% APR costs approximately $1,000 in interest over 21 months if minimum payments are made. Even with the 5% fee, the BankAmericard saves money. Calculate your specific savings before transferring.

No Penalty APR — Forgiving Structure

The BankAmericard does not charge a penalty APR — if you miss a payment, your interest rate won't jump to 29.99% as it does on many cards. You'll still pay a late fee and your credit score may be impacted if more than 30 days late, but the APR itself stays unchanged. This is unusually consumer-friendly and particularly valuable for cardholders using the card to pay down debt who may be managing tight cash flow.

No Rewards — Use as a Temporary Tool, Not a Daily Driver

The BankAmericard earns zero rewards on purchases — no cash back, no points, no miles. Once the 21-month 0% period ends, this card has no ongoing value for everyday spending. The recommended approach: use it exclusively to eliminate debt or finance a specific large purchase during the intro period, then switch to a rewards-earning card for all daily spending. Keep the BankAmericard open after the intro period (closing it would shorten your average account age and potentially hurt your credit score), but don't spend on it.

Bottom Line

The BankAmericard is a single-purpose tool — and it's the best tool available for its specific job. If you have credit card debt on a high-rate card and want the maximum interest-free runway to pay it off, 21 billing cycles without interest is hard to beat. The 5% transfer fee is worth paying if your existing rate is above ~3%. After the intro period expires, stop spending on it and switch to a rewards-earning card. Keep the account open to preserve your credit history.
