The Discover it® Student Cash Back is one of the best first credit cards available — period. It offers the exact same 5% rotating categories and unlimited Cashback Match as the standard Discover it Cash Back, but is specifically designed for students with no credit history. No parent cosigner required, no prior credit needed, and Discover reports to all three major bureaus to help build your credit file from day one.

Good Grade Reward — $20/Year for Academic Performance

Each school year that you provide proof of a GPA of 3.0 or higher, Discover credits $20 to your account. This applies for up to 5 years — $100 total if your GPA stays at 3.0+ throughout college. To claim: log in to your Discover account and submit your GPA certificate or transcript. This is a genuinely unusual benefit that rewards responsible academic habits alongside responsible credit habits.

Building Credit as a Student

Discover reports to all three major credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) monthly, meaning your on-time payments build a credit history with all three. The card also provides free FICO Score access monthly — important for students learning how their credit behavior affects their score. Discover offers a free dark web monitoring alert if your Social Security number appears on risky sites, a useful security feature for students who may be less experienced with identity protection.

No Credit History Required — But Responsible Use Is Critical

Discover designed this card for students who are completely new to credit. Approval is based primarily on income (including financial aid, part-time job earnings, or regular allowances) and basic eligibility, not prior credit history. The most important thing after getting approved: pay your statement balance in full every month. The APR (16.74%–25.74%) is slightly lower than the standard card's — carrying a balance erases the value of any cash back earned.

Student Cash Back vs. Student Chrome

Discover offers two student cards: this rotating-category card and the Discover it Student Chrome (2% at gas stations and restaurants, 1% elsewhere). The Student Cash Back is better for students who will remember to activate quarterly categories and can hit the 5% maximum in categories like groceries (Q1) and restaurants (Q2). The Student Chrome is better for students who want a simpler card that earns 2% automatically at gas and restaurants without any activation.

Bottom Line

The Discover it Student Cash Back is the best first credit card for most college students. It offers genuine rewards — not a restricted version — at zero annual cost, no credit history required. The Cashback Match makes year one exceptionally valuable, and the Good Grade Reward ties financial and academic habits together. Apply when starting college, use it for groceries and everyday expenses, activate categories each quarter, and pay in full every month. By graduation you'll have a solid credit history, meaningful cash back earnings, and a clear path to premium cards.
