The Capital One Venture X is the most straightforward case for a premium travel card on the market. Every year, you get $300 in travel credits plus 10,000 anniversary miles worth $100+ — combined, $400+ in annual value that exceeds the $395 annual fee before earning a single point on purchases. In practice this card costs nothing to hold.

Stack on top of that: Priority Pass lounge access for you (guests now pay a per-visit fee), 2x miles on every purchase with no category complexity, and 75,000 miles welcome bonus — and you have the clearest value proposition in the premium travel card category.

Sign-Up Bonus: 75,000 Miles

New cardholders earn 75,000 bonus miles after spending $4,000 on purchases in the first 3 months. At Capital One's 1¢ per mile value for travel redemptions, that's worth $750 toward travel. Use transfer partners well and the value goes higher — Capital One transfers to Air Canada Aeroplan, Turkish Miles&Smiles, and other partners at 1:1 ratios where values of 1.5–2¢+ per point are achievable.

Annual Credits: The Fee Justifier

The $300 travel credit applies to bookings made through Capital One Travel portal — flights, hotels, rental cars. The 10,000 anniversary miles post each year on your account anniversary. Together they make the card essentially self-funding for anyone who books any travel at all.

2x on Everything Is the Real Feature

No other premium card matches 2x on all purchases. The Amex Platinum earns 1x on non-bonus spending. The Chase Sapphire Reserve earns 1x. The Venture X's 2x baseline means you never leave rewards on the table — every dollar you spend earns double miles, no exceptions.

Lounge Access: Priority Pass + Capital One Lounges

⚠️ Guest Lounge Access Changed February 1, 2026

Capital One eliminated free guest access to Priority Pass lounges and Capital One Lounges effective February 1, 2026. Cardholders retain unlimited personal lounge access at no cost. To bring guests to Capital One Lounges or Landing locations for free, you must spend $75,000 on the card in a calendar year. Priority Pass guests now pay a per-visit fee regardless of spend. This is a notable downgrade versus prior terms.

Cardholders receive Priority Pass Select membership — access to 1,300+ airport lounges worldwide with no per-visit fees for the cardholder. Note: As of February 1, 2026, free guest access has been eliminated. Guests now require a per-visit fee for Priority Pass lounges. Free guest access to Capital One Lounges and Landing locations is only available after $75,000 in annual card spending. Capital One Lounges are currently open in Dallas, Denver, Washington Dulles, Las Vegas, and New York JFK, where cardholders get free access with premium food, cocktails, and spa services (guests $45 per adult unless you hit the $75,000 spend threshold).

Transfer Partners: 15+ Programs

Capital One miles transfer to 15+ airline and hotel partners including Air Canada Aeroplan, Turkish Miles&Smiles, Avianca LifeMiles, British Airways Avios, Flying Blue, and Wyndham Rewards. Most transfers are at 1:1 ratios. Turkish Miles&Smiles and Avianca in particular offer outsized value for Star Alliance business class redemptions.

The Bottom Line

The Capital One Venture X delivers a simple, powerful argument: it effectively costs nothing to hold after year one, yet delivers Priority Pass lounge access, 2x on all purchases, and a robust transfer partner ecosystem. For travelers who want premium benefits without paying a net annual fee and without the complexity of category bonuses, there is no better card.

It's not the right choice if you need Centurion Lounge access (Amex Platinum) or Hyatt transfer value (Chase Sapphire). But for straightforward premium travel value at the lowest net cost, the Venture X wins outright.
