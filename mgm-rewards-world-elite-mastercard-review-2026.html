The MGM Rewards World Elite Mastercard is the no-annual-fee MGM Resorts card from FNBO (First National Bank of Omaha). It earns MGM Mastercard Points and MGM Rewards Tier Credits on every purchase, upgrades you to Pearl status automatically and keeps you at Pearl or higher while the account is open and in good standing. It sits below the $249 MGM Rewards Iconic World Elite Mastercard, which earns more and adds annual credits.

Every rate above earns both points and Tier Credits per $1. Points can be redeemed for SLOT DOLLARS® and MGM Rewards Points, and they don't expire as long as you keep using the card. FNBO doesn't list a welcome offer on its card comparison page, so check the application page for the current offer before you apply.

Benefits

  • Automatic Pearl status: your MGM Rewards tier stays at least Pearl while the account is open and in good standing.
  • Free self-parking at participating MGM Rewards destinations (insert your MGM Rewards card at the exit gate; subject to availability).
  • No foreign transaction fees.
  • World Elite Mastercard benefits, Mastercard Zero Liability protection and Mastercard ID Theft Protection.

World Elite vs. Iconic

The Iconic card doubles the on-property earn rate and adds a $200 annual resort credit, a Global Entry or TSA PreCheck credit, and a Priority Pass digital membership, for $249 a year. If you stay at MGM properties several times a year, see our Iconic review. For occasional visitors, the free World Elite card gets you Pearl status and free self-parking without a fee.

Bottom Line

The MGM Rewards World Elite Mastercard is an easy card to keep if you visit MGM Resorts properties: it costs nothing, holds you at Pearl status, saves on parking and earns points and Tier Credits on everything you buy. Its everyday earn rates are modest, so pair it with a general-purpose rewards card, and look at the Iconic card if you spend enough at MGM properties to use its annual credits.
