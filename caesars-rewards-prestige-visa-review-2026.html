The Caesars Rewards Prestige Visa Signature is the paid version of the Caesars Rewards credit card. Compared with the no-fee Caesars Rewards Visa Signature, it earns more at Caesars Rewards destinations (7x instead of 5x), comes with three yearly on-property perks, offers a larger welcome bonus, adds extra Tier Credit milestones and drops the foreign transaction fee. You can apply for it directly, and existing Caesars Rewards Visa cardholders may be able to upgrade from their Comenity account.

The welcome bonus requires $1,000 in purchases outside Caesars Rewards destinations within 90 days of account opening. Reward Credits are redeemed within the Caesars Rewards program (hotel stays, dining, entertainment and play at participating properties), so their value depends on how and where you use them.

If you use all three, the stated value comfortably exceeds the $149 fee. If you visit a Caesars property less than once a year, you will likely leave most of it on the table, and the no-fee card is the better choice.

Status and Tier Credits

Using the card within 90 days of approval upgrades you to Caesars Rewards Platinum status. The Prestige card also earns spend-based Tier Credit bonuses: 2,500 Tier Credits at $5,000 in annual spend and another 2,500 at $10,000. Caesars' card page lists a further 15,000 bonus at $50,000 in annual spend, but its tables disagree on whether that bonus is paid in Tier Credits or Reward Credits. Confirm the current terms with Comenity before you plan a status run around it.

Prestige vs. the No-Fee Caesars Visa

The upgrade is mostly about the annual perks. The extra 2x at Caesars destinations only adds up for people who spend heavily on property. If you stay at a Caesars Rewards destination at least once a year and would use the dining credit and free night anyway, Prestige wins. Otherwise, keep the no-annual-fee version.

Bottom Line

The Caesars Rewards Prestige Visa Signature makes sense for people who already visit Caesars, Harrah's, Horseshoe or other Caesars Rewards properties at least once a year. The yearly dining credit, slot play and anniversary night can cover the $149 fee on their own, and the 7x on-property earn rate and missing foreign transaction fee are useful extras. Occasional visitors should stick with the no-fee Caesars Rewards Visa Signature or a flexible rewards card.
