The PayPal Cashback Mastercard earns 3% on any purchase where you select PayPal as the payment method at checkout — which covers a surprisingly wide swath of online and in-person spending. PayPal is accepted at Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Airbnb, American Airlines, United, Netflix, Etsy, DoorDash, Uber, and millions of other merchants. For habitual PayPal users, this card converts existing checkout behavior into a 3% cash back stream at zero annual cost.

Where 3% Applies — PayPal's Merchant Network

Major merchants and platforms offering PayPal checkout where you earn 3%:

  • E-commerce: Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, Best Buy, Target, Wayfair
  • Travel: Airbnb, American Airlines, United Airlines, Hotels.com, Priceline, Booking.com
  • Streaming + subscriptions: Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, Spotify
  • Food + delivery: DoorDash, Instacart, Grubhub, Uber Eats
  • Rideshare: Uber
  • In-store QR: Select merchants accepting PayPal QR code payments

Instant Redemption to PayPal Balance

Cash back is earned daily and deposited to your PayPal balance automatically, with no minimum redemption amount. From your PayPal balance you can: spend via PayPal anywhere PayPal is accepted, transfer to your bank, send to other PayPal users via the app, or use on the PayPal Debit card. This is one of the fastest and most flexible cash back redemption structures available.

PayPal Account Required — Evaluate Your Actual PayPal Usage

Before applying, estimate what percentage of your monthly spending actually goes through PayPal checkout. If you rarely use PayPal, the 1.5% base rate is your effective earn rate — which trails the Wells Fargo Active Cash (2%) and Citi Double Cash (2%) on non-PayPal spending. The card only delivers its headline 3% value for consistent PayPal users.

Bottom Line

The PayPal Cashback Mastercard is the best single card for online shoppers who route most of their purchases through PayPal. The 3% rate with no cap is excellent for a no-fee card. The ideal setup: PayPal Cashback for all PayPal transactions, Wells Fargo Active Cash for non-PayPal in-store and everyday spending. Together they form a simple two-card cash back system that captures 3% online and 2% everywhere else.
