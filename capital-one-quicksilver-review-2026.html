The Capital One Quicksilver is the flagship no-annual-fee cash back card in Capital One's lineup. It earns a flat 1.5% on every purchase with no caps, no categories, no activation, and no foreign transaction fees — plus 5% on hotels, vacation rentals, and rental cars booked through Capital One Travel. The $200 welcome bonus after just $500 in spending is one of the easiest bonuses in the no-fee cash back category.

The Quicksilver sits in an interesting competitive position: it's simpler than the Chase Freedom Flex (no activation required) but earns less than the Wells Fargo Active Cash (1.5% vs. 2%) on everyday spending. Its best case is as a set-it-and-forget-it card for Capital One customers, especially paired with the Venture or Venture X to unlock transfer partners.

2026 Network Change: Select Accounts Moving to Discover

Following Capital One's 2025 acquisition of Discover Financial, Capital One began transitioning select Quicksilver cardholders from Mastercard to the Discover network in May 2026. Affected cardholders received email notification and new cards. Those transitioned also received a new 3% cash back category on gas stations and grocery stores — a meaningful bonus. Cardholders who were not notified remain on Mastercard with the standard 1.5% flat rate. New applicants as of April 2026 should check current offer terms at Capital One's website for the applicable network and category structure.

Quicksilver vs. Wells Fargo Active Cash

The most common Quicksilver comparison. Both are no-annual-fee flat-rate cards — but the Active Cash earns 2% vs. Quicksilver's 1.5%, and the Active Cash offers $600 cell phone protection. On $20,000 in annual spending, the Active Cash earns $100 more per year. The Quicksilver wins on: no foreign transaction fee (Active Cash charges 3%), brand preference within Capital One's ecosystem, and pairing with Venture/Venture X cards to pool and transfer rewards to airline partners.

Best Pairing: Quicksilver + Capital One Venture

The Quicksilver earns non-transferable cash back on its own. But if you also hold the Capital One Venture ($95/yr), you can combine cash back rewards from both cards and transfer to Capital One's 15+ airline and hotel partners at 1:1. The Quicksilver fills in 1.5% on all non-travel purchases; the Venture handles the 2x everything floor. Pairing them creates a strong no-fee + fee card setup for travel redemptions.

Bottom Line

The Capital One Quicksilver is a solid no-fee cash back card — especially for Capital One loyalists who pair it with a Venture card for transfer partner access. Its 1.5% base rate is competitive and the no foreign transaction fee is a genuine advantage over most flat-rate alternatives. For pure cash back maximization on domestic spending, the Wells Fargo Active Cash earns 33% more (2% vs. 1.5%) at the same $0 annual fee. Choose Quicksilver if you value Capital One's ecosystem, travel perks, or international use.
