The Delta SkyMiles Reserve is the top-tier card in Delta's personal credit card lineup. At $650/year, it's a serious commitment — but for frequent Delta flyers, the combination of Sky Club + Centurion Lounge access, an annual companion certificate worth hundreds to thousands of dollars, and a fast track to Medallion elite status makes it one of the most valuable airline cards on the market.

The key question is whether you fly Delta enough to use the benefits. The companion certificate alone can be worth $500–$2,000+ depending on the route and cabin. The lounge access is among the best available on any credit card. If you fly Delta 4+ times a year and check bags, the case for the Reserve is compelling.

Welcome Offer: Up to 100,000 Miles

New cardholders can earn up to 100,000 bonus miles after spending $5,000 on eligible purchases in the first 6 months. Welcome offers vary — Amex shows your personalized offer at application with no credit score impact until you accept.

At TPG's April 2026 valuation of 1.2¢ per SkyMile, 100,000 miles are worth $1,200 toward Delta flights — enough to cover the annual fee in year one and then some. The $5,000 spend requirement over 6 months ($833/month) is moderate for a premium card.

Once-Per-Lifetime Amex Rule

Amex enforces a strict once-per-lifetime welcome bonus policy. If you've previously earned a welcome offer on the Delta SkyMiles Reserve, you won't qualify for the welcome bonus again. Additionally, Amex's "family language" rules mean that holding the Delta Gold or Platinum may affect your Reserve eligibility — check before applying.

Lounge Access: Sky Club + Centurion

The Delta Reserve's lounge package is one of the most extensive available on any airline co-branded credit card:

✈️

Delta Sky Club — 15 Visits/Year

Access to Delta's premium airport lounges — 50+ clubs worldwide — with food, drinks, and Wi-Fi. After 15 visits, additional visits cost $50 each. Spend $75,000 on the card in a year to unlock unlimited visits. Four guest passes included annually ($50/guest after that).

👑

Centurion Lounges + Escape Lounges

Complimentary access to American Express Centurion Lounges (widely considered the best credit card lounges, with full bar, hot food, and spa services) and Escape Lounges when flying Delta booked with your Reserve card. Two guests cost $50 each per visit.

Important: Basic Economy Restriction

You cannot access the Delta Sky Club or claim lounge benefits if you're flying on a Basic Economy ticket — even as a Reserve cardholder. This restriction applies to Sky Club, Centurion Lounge, and Escape Lounge access. Always purchase at least Main Cabin to use your lounge benefits.

Annual Companion Certificate: The Headliner Benefit

Every year after your card renewal, you receive a Companion Certificate valid for a round-trip flight in First Class, Delta Comfort+, or Main Cabin within the U.S. and to Mexico, the Caribbean, or Central America. You pay for one ticket; your companion flies on the same booking for just taxes and fees ($22–$250 depending on routing).

The value depends entirely on which ticket you buy and the route. A First Class ticket from New York to Los Angeles can run $800–$2,000+ — your companion flies for $22 in taxes. Even on a modest $300 domestic Main Cabin ticket, the certificate saves $278+ net. This single benefit frequently covers or exceeds the entire $650 annual fee.

Maximizing the Companion Certificate

Book in First Class on a long domestic route during peak travel season for maximum value. New York to Los Angeles, Boston to Seattle, or Miami to Honolulu in First Class can yield $1,000–$2,000+ in companion savings. The certificate is valid for up to 1 year after issuance — time your renewal and use it strategically.

The Reserve earns its keep through its lounge access, companion certificate, and status benefits — not through points accumulation on everyday spending.

Medallion Status Acceleration

The Reserve provides two Medallion-boosting benefits:

  • MQD Headstart: $2,500 in Medallion Qualification Dollars deposited into your SkyMiles account each Medallion Year — giving you a head start toward Silver ($3,000 MQDs), Gold ($6,000), Platinum ($9,000), or Diamond ($15,000) status.
  • MQD Boost: Earn $1 MQD for every $10 spent on the card. Spend $20,000 and earn $2,000 additional MQDs. Combined with the $2,500 headstart, that's $4,500 MQDs from card spending alone — covering half of Gold status requirements without flying a single mile.

Other Benefits

  • TakeOff 15: 15% off all Delta award flights booked with SkyMiles — same benefit as the Gold card, applies permanently
  • First checked bag free for cardholder and up to 8 companions on the same reservation
  • Priority boarding in Zone 5 on all Delta flights
  • Complimentary upgrades: Reserve cardholders receive upgrade priority above other Medallion members at the same tier and fare class
  • Hertz President's Circle status: Top-tier Hertz rental car status, which includes guaranteed upgrades and expedited service
  • No foreign transaction fees
  • Amex purchase protections: Extended warranty, purchase protection, return protection

Who Should Get the Delta Reserve?

Get it if you:

  • Fly Delta 4+ times per year and value lounge access — the Sky Club + Centurion combination is genuinely premium
  • Travel with a partner or companion and can use the annual First Class companion certificate for maximum value
  • Are working toward Silver, Gold, or Platinum Medallion status and want the $2,500 MQD headstart
  • Currently pay for lounge day passes — at $50/visit, the 15 Sky Club visits alone are worth $750/year

Skip it if you:

  • Fly Delta only 1–2 times per year — the Delta Gold is the better value at that frequency
  • Fly across multiple airlines — the Amex Platinum ($895) provides Centurion Lounges plus 20+ transfer partners including Delta
  • Don't have a travel companion for the certificate — half the annual fee value disappears

The Bottom Line

The Delta SkyMiles Reserve is a premium card for premium Delta travelers. The companion certificate is the defining benefit — it regularly delivers more value than the entire $650 annual fee in a single redemption for First Class travel. Add Sky Club + Centurion Lounge access, $560+ in annual credits, and MQD status acceleration, and this card pays for itself several times over for anyone who flies Delta frequently and travels with a companion.

For occasional Delta flyers, the Delta Gold at $150/year is the smarter choice. But for Delta loyalists who value elite status and premium lounge access, the Reserve has no peer in the airline card category.
