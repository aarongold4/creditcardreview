The Citi Strata Premier is the best $95 travel card for everyday spenders. While Chase Sapphire Preferred earns 3x on dining and travel, the Strata Premier earns 3x across five categories — restaurants, supermarkets, gas/EV, air travel, and hotels direct — covering more of most households' monthly spending. The $100 annual hotel benefit alone covers the annual fee. And the exclusive AAdvantage transfer partnership makes this the essential card for American Airlines loyalists.

$100 Annual Hotel Benefit — Covers the Fee

Book a single hotel stay of $500 or more through cititravel.com once per calendar year and receive $100 off instantly at booking. This makes the Strata Premier effectively fee-neutral for anyone booking even one $500+ hotel stay annually — which is a single two-night weekend at a mid-range city hotel. You also earn 10x on the remaining ~$400 balance of the booking, worth another $40+ in points value.

22 Transfer Partners at 1:1 — AAdvantage Exclusive

ThankYou Points transfer 1:1 to 22 airline and hotel partners. American Airlines AAdvantage is exclusively a Citi ThankYou partner — Chase, Amex, and Capital One do not offer AAdvantage transfers. Other strong partners include Air France-KLM Flying Blue (excellent for transatlantic business class), Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer, Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles, and Avianca LifeMiles. Hotel partners include Choice Hotels, Wyndham Rewards, and Leading Hotels of the World.

Best Value: Transfer to AAdvantage or Flying Blue

At 1.13 cents/point estimated value through transfers, 70,000 ThankYou points are worth $678+ in travel via partners. AA saver awards on domestic routes start at 7,500 miles one-way; Flying Blue frequently runs promo awards. Transferring to a partner rather than redeeming at 1¢/pt through the portal significantly increases the value of your welcome bonus.

Bottom Line

The Citi Strata Premier earns the top rating among $95 travel cards for everyday spenders. The breadth of 3x categories — especially supermarkets and gas, which most $95 travel cards ignore — makes it a strong daily driver. The $100 hotel benefit neutralizes the annual fee each year for anyone booking one hotel stay. The exclusive AAdvantage partnership makes it essential for American Airlines loyalists. The main gap versus CSP is the absence of Hyatt transfers and primary rental car insurance. For AA flyers and grocery-heavy households, the Strata Premier wins.
