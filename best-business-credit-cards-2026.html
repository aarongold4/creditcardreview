Best Business Credit Cards of 2026: Expert Reviews for Small Business Owners

Whether you run a freelance operation or a growing small business, the right business credit card earns rewards on every expense, separates personal and business finances, and can put hundreds or thousands of dollars back in your pocket every year.

Fact-checked

You don't need a registered LLC or a large team to get a business credit card. Freelancers, consultants, Etsy sellers, Uber drivers, and anyone with a side hustle can qualify. And the benefits go well beyond rewards — a dedicated business card separates your expenses for tax purposes, builds business credit, and often comes with higher credit limits and stronger purchase protections than personal cards.

Who Qualifies for a Business Credit Card?

Any individual with a source of self-employment income can apply — including freelancers, contractors, gig workers, and side hustlers. You don't need an EIN. You can apply using your Social Security number as a sole proprietor.

How to Choose the Right Business Credit Card

Step 1 — Identify your top two spending categories

Look at your last three months of business expenses. Where does most of your money go? Common categories include advertising (Google/Meta ads), telecom and software subscriptions, travel, shipping, office supplies, and dining with clients. Your top category determines your best card.

Step 2 — Decide on annual fee tolerance

If your business spends less than $25,000 per year on credit cards, a no-annual-fee card almost always wins. The Ink Business Cash or Amex Blue Business Cash will outperform fee-based cards at that spending level. Above $50,000/year, a card like the Ink Preferred or Amex Business Gold starts generating enough extra rewards to justify the annual fee.

Step 3 — Consider the sign-up bonus timing

Time your application around a period when you know you'll have higher-than-normal spending — a large equipment purchase, a busy season, or a planned advertising push. Meeting the sign-up bonus spend threshold through normal business expenses is the key to unlocking the full bonus value.

The Two-Card Business Strategy

Many small business owners use two cards together: the Ink Business Cash for 5% on telecom and office supplies, paired with the Ink Business Unlimited for 1.5% on everything else. Both have no annual fee, both earn Ultimate Rewards points that transfer to partners when combined with an Ink Preferred or Sapphire card, and together they maximize rewards across all business spending.

The Bottom Line

For most small businesses, the Ink Business Preferred is the best overall choice — its 100,000-point bonus, 3x categories covering the most common business expenses, and $95 annual fee make it the single highest-value business card available right now.

If you want zero annual fee, the Ink Business Cash is the best option for service businesses with significant telecom spend, while the Amex Blue Business Cash wins for flat-rate simplicity at 2% on everything.

And remember — if you're a freelancer or side hustler who's never applied for a business card, you almost certainly qualify. Apply with your Social Security number as a sole proprietor and you'll be earning business rewards on every expense you're already making.
