The Discover it® Miles is the travel-focused version of Discover's no-annual-fee lineup. It earns 1.5x miles on everything (equal to 1.5% toward travel), with no categories, no activation, and the same Miles Match in year one that doubles all miles earned. Miles redeem as statement credits against any travel purchase — flights, hotels, taxis, trains, Airbnb — with no portal required and no blackout dates.

Miles are worth 1 cent each. 1.5x miles = 1.5% back toward travel. The redemption is simple: any travel purchase on your statement (booked anywhere, any airline, any hotel) becomes eligible for a miles redemption as a statement credit up to the purchase amount.

Redemption — No Portal, No Restrictions

This is the card's biggest differentiator. Most travel cards require booking through the issuer's portal to get good value, or force you through a complex transfer-and-redeem process. Discover it Miles redeems differently: book your travel however you like (directly with the airline, on Expedia, through Airbnb, via Uber), then log into your Discover account and apply miles against that charge as a statement credit. You can also redeem for cash at the same 1 cent per mile, or with PayPal at select merchants.com, though travel redemptions provide the best value at 1 cent per mile.

Miles Match in Year One — The Core Value Proposition

The Miles Match doubles all miles earned at the end of your first 12 billing cycles. On $20,000 in spending at 1.5x = 30,000 miles ($300 toward travel). Miles Match adds another 30,000 miles ($300). Total first-year value: $600 toward travel from a $0-annual-fee card. This rivals many $95-annual-fee travel cards in first-year value — especially compared to cards with no welcome bonus.

vs. Capital One Quicksilver and Wells Fargo Active Cash

All three are no-annual-fee flat-rate cards. The Active Cash earns 2% redeemable as cash with no restrictions — more flexible than travel-only miles. The Quicksilver earns 1.5% with no foreign transaction fee. The Discover it Miles earns 1.5x with the Miles Match advantage in year one and no foreign transaction fee. After year one, the Active Cash's 2% rate wins for pure cash flexibility; the Discover Miles wins for travelers who want simple statement credit redemption against any travel purchase without foreign transaction fees.

Bottom Line

The Discover it Miles is the best no-fee travel card for simplicity seekers who want 1.5x on everything, flexible travel redemptions with no portal required, and a strong first-year bonus through Miles Match. It doesn't have transfer partners or high category multipliers — but it never requires you to think about booking channels or redemption strategy. For frequent travelers building their first travel card wallet, it's an excellent starting point before graduating to a card with airline transfer partners.
