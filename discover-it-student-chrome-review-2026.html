The Discover it® Student Chrome is the simpler of the two student Discover cards. Where the Student Cash Back requires quarterly activation to unlock 5% on rotating categories, the Student Chrome earns 2% at gas stations and restaurants automatically — no activation, no tracking, no quarterly decisions. For students who drive regularly, eat out often, or simply don't want to manage a card's bonus categories, the Chrome is the better starting card.

The Student Cash Back earns significantly more for students who will activate every quarter and spend in the right categories. The Student Chrome is the better card for students who are new to managing a credit card and don't want to add "activate Discover categories" to their calendar. Both share the Cashback Match year one, making first-year value strong regardless of which you choose.

Building Credit with the Student Chrome

Discover reports your payment history and balance to all three major credit bureaus monthly. Using the card regularly for gas and dining, then paying the full statement balance every month, builds a positive credit history across Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion simultaneously. Discover also provides free FICO Score access through your account starting around month three — letting you track your progress as a tangible metric of responsible credit use.

Good Grade Reward — Same as Student Cash Back

Submit your GPA of 3.0 or higher each school year and receive a $20 statement credit. This applies for up to 5 years = up to $100 total. Log in to your Discover account and submit your transcript or grade report. It's a small but genuinely unusual benefit that ties responsible financial habits to academic performance.

Bottom Line

The Discover it Student Chrome is the right first credit card for students who want a simple, automatic rewards structure without managing quarterly categories. The Cashback Match makes year one strong, the Good Grade Reward ties academic performance to financial benefits, and the $0 annual fee means there's zero cost to building credit responsibly. Students who are comfortable activating quarterly categories should consider the Student Cash Back instead — it earns significantly more for active optimizers. But for the set-and-forget student cardholder, Chrome is the cleaner, lower-maintenance choice.
