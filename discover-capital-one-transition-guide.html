Discover Cards Under Capital One: What Changes for Cardholders in 2026–2027

Your Discover card is moving to Capital One's app and website. Here's what changes, what stays the same, and the few things worth doing before your account moves.

📅 Updated November 22, 2026
✍️ By Nathan Blake
⏳ 7 min read
✓ Fact-checked

Very little changes about the card itself. Starting July 27, 2026, Capital One began moving Discover credit cards onto its own website, app and customer service, in waves that continue into 2027. Your card stays on the Discover network, primary cardholders keep the same card number, there's still no annual fee, and your rewards balance, earning rates and first-year match carry over. What you gain is access to Capital One Travel, Capital One Entertainment and Capital One Offers. What you lose, briefly, is the ability to redeem rewards or update your account while the move happens.

What Changes and What Stays the Same

The Short Version

Think of it as a new landlord, not a new card. Discover's rewards stay; Capital One's portals get added on top; and you'll log in somewhere else.

ItemAfter your account moves to Capital One
Payment networkStays the same. Capital One says the Discover brand and network are not going away.

Rewards balanceCarries over and doesn't expire for the life of the account.
Earning ratesStay the same. 5% rotating categories still require activation each quarter; an activation made before the move stays active for that quarter.
Cashback Match / Miles MatchContinues for rewards earned in your first year.
Annual feeStill $0.
Credit historyUnaffected. It isn't reported as a new account, and your open date stays the same.
Where you manage the cardChanges. Capital One's website and app replace Discover's, and Capital One handles customer service.
Capital One TravelNew. 5% cash back (5 miles per dollar on Miles cards) on hotels, rental cars and activities booked through the portal.
Capital One EntertainmentNew. 5% cash back (5 miles per dollar on Miles cards) on tickets and events.
Capital One OffersNew. Up to 15% cash back (or up to 15X miles) on online shopping with participating retailers.
APR and minimum paymentListed in the account terms Capital One sends by email or mail. Read them rather than assuming.

The new portal earnings are a genuine upgrade on paper, especially for Discover it Cash Back and Chrome holders who had no travel category before. But 5% through a portal is only worth it if the portal's price is competitive. Check the same hotel or rental car directly before you book.

The Transition Window

While your account is being moved, you can't redeem rewards or update account details. Both come back once the move is done. Don't plan a big redemption for the weekend your notice names.

Card by Card: What Each Discover Card Looks Like Now

Discover it Cash Back

The flagship keeps its 5% rotating categories on up to $1,500 a quarter with activation, 1% elsewhere, and the unlimited first-year Cashback Match. New cardholders also get a $200 statement credit after $500 in purchases in the first 3 months (on Discover's offer page as of November 2026); that credit itself isn't matched. Q4 2026 categories are entertainment, restaurants and utilities through December 31. No 2027 category calendar has been announced yet. Full details in our Discover it Cash Back review.

Discover it Chrome

Still 2% at gas stations and restaurants on the first $1,000 of combined spending each quarter, then 1%. The one thing to know: Discover's application disclosures for the Chrome — and also for the it Cash Back, NHL, Student Chrome and Miles cards — say that if you're approved through that page, some of your account terms will change in January 2027. The specifics are in the Amended Account Terms, not on the offer page, so read them before you apply. See our Discover it Chrome review.

Discover it Miles

The Miles card keeps 1.5x on everything and the first-year Miles Match, and picks up 5x on Capital One Travel bookings. That's the best-matched new perk in the lineup, because Miles holders are already redeeming against travel. Our Discover it Miles review has the rest.

Discover it Secured Cash Back

This one did change. Discover stopped taking applications for the old Discover it Secured in June 2026, and Capital One relaunched it as the Discover it Secured Cash Back on October 5, 2026. The new version asks for a $49, $99 or $200 deposit based on creditworthiness, gives at least a $200 credit line, and earns the same 5% rotating categories as the unsecured card, plus the first-year Cashback Match. Read our Discover it Secured Cash Back review for the full comparison with the old card.

Discover it Student Cash Back and Student Chrome

Student cards are on the same migration path, with the same earning structures as their non-student versions: 5% rotating categories on the Student Cash Back, 2% gas and restaurants on the Student Chrome. Wait for the email that names your date. See our Student Cash Back review and Student Chrome review.

NHL Discover it

Underneath the team design, this is a Discover it Cash Back: same 5% categories, same Cashback Match, same $0 fee. Capital One's migration FAQ doesn't single it out. The one difference worth noting is that the NHL card's page doesn't show the standard card's $200 welcome credit. Details in our NHL Discover it review.

Traffic runs the other way too: Capital One now issues some of its own cards on the Discover network. Check the network logo on your card if you're unsure. Our Capital One Quicksilver review covers what that means for Quicksilver holders.

What to Do Now

There's nothing to do until Capital One emails you a migration notice. When it arrives:

  1. Set up your Capital One login when prompted.
  2. Activate this quarter's categories on a rotating-category card if you haven't. Activation isn't retroactive, and an activation made before the move carries over.
  3. Read the account terms Capital One sends. That's where your APR and minimum payment are listed.
  4. Redeem early or wait. Rewards are frozen during the move, not lost. If you need cash back for a specific bill, redeem before your conversion date.
  5. Update authorized user cards anywhere they're saved. Authorized users and joint holders get new numbers; primary cardholders don't.
  6. Recheck digital wallets. Apple Pay recurring subscriptions may need to be set up again.
  7. Confirm your first scheduled payment. Scheduled and automatic payments are meant to transfer. If your account is set up on the Monday after conversion weekend, recent payments can take up to 3 business days to appear; Capital One honors the date it received them.
  8. Sign up for CreditWise if you used Discover's free credit monitoring. CreditWise is Capital One's equivalent.

Should You Close Your Discover Card?

No. Nothing announced so far cuts the card's earning rates, the account keeps its age, and it adds portal earnings. Closing a no-fee card only shrinks your available credit.

Discover to Capital One FAQ

Will my Discover card stop working?

No. Primary cardholders keep using their current card and number. Authorized users and joint holders can keep using their current card until the new one arrives.

Is my Discover card becoming a Visa or Mastercard?

No. Capital One says the Discover brand and network are not going away, and migrated cards stay on Discover.

When will my account move?

Capital One moves accounts when they're ready, and if you hold several Discover cards they may move on different dates. You'll get an email or letter with your date beforehand.

Does the move hurt my credit score?

No. It isn't reported as a new account, and your account open date stays the same.

Are the 2027 5% categories out yet?

Not as of November 16, 2026. Discover has published categories through Q4 2026 only.

The Bottom Line

For almost every Discover cardholder, the Capital One move is a net positive: same card, same rewards, plus 5% portal categories and Capital One Offers. The two things that deserve real attention are the unpublished January 2027 term changes that Discover's application disclosures flag for new applicants and the short window when rewards are locked. Everything else is a new login. If you're shopping for a no-fee card, our best cash back cards and Discover it vs. Chase Freedom Flex comparison are the next stops.
