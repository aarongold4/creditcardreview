The NHL Discover it® card is Discover's co-branded NHL credit card, available in team-specific designs for most NHL franchises. Mechanically, it is identical to the standard Discover it® Cash Back — the same 5% on rotating quarterly categories, 1% on everything else, and unlimited Cashback Match in year one. The differentiation is in the brand experience: NHL team designs on the card itself, and access to NHL-related fan experiences and perks through the Discover NHL partnership.

The 2026 quarterly categories are the same as the standard Discover it Cash Back: Q1 groceries/streaming/wholesale clubs, Q2 restaurants/home improvement, Q3 gas stations/EV charging, public transportation, flights and drugstores; Q4 entertainment, restaurants and utilities. Activation is required each quarter.

NHL Fan Perks

Beyond the standard Discover it rewards structure, the NHL Discover it card provides:

  • Team-specific card designs — available for most NHL franchises, letting you carry your team's branding
  • NHL fan experiences — access to exclusive events, ticket presales, and game-day experiences through the Discover-NHL partnership
  • NHL-branded rewards — some redemption options for NHL merchandise, memorabilia, or event packages through Discover's rewards portal

The fan perks are the primary reason to choose the NHL card over the standard Discover it — the underlying rewards structure is identical. If NHL brand identity and fan access matter to you, this card delivers them at no additional cost.

Functionally the Same as Discover it Cash Back

The NHL Discover it is mechanically identical to the standard Discover it Cash Back. Same earn rates, same Cashback Match, same APR range, same $0 annual fee. The differences are the team-branded card design, NHL fan perks, and the standard card's new $200 welcome bonus, which the NHL card's page does not show. If the NHL branding isn't a draw for you, the standard Discover it Cash Back or Discover it Chrome may be a cleaner choice depending on your spending patterns.

Bottom Line

The NHL Discover it is a great choice for hockey fans who want strong cash back rewards with their team's branding. The underlying card is the same best-in-class rotating category product as the standard Discover it Cash Back — adding NHL fan perks on top at no extra cost. If you're choosing between the NHL card and the standard Discover it, the rewards are identical: pick based on whether you want the NHL brand experience. If you're a hockey fan getting into credit cards, this card is a legitimate first-wallet choice.
