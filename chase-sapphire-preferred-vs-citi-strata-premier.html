📅 Updated June 12, 2026 — CSP Card Refresh

The Chase Sapphire Preferred was refreshed effective June 15, 2026. Key updates reflected in this comparison: standard welcome bonus 75,000 pts after $5,000/3mo (a limited-time 100,000-pt launch offer ended July 30, 2026); hotel credit doubled to $100/yr; new 3x on gas/EV and vacation rentals; Hyatt transfer ratio reduced to 4:3 (new applicants since June 15, 2026; all cardmembers since Oct 1, 2026).

Two of the best $95 travel cards available in 2026, the Chase Sapphire Preferred and Citi Strata Premier are closer competitors than most people realize. Both charge $95/year, both earn 3x on dining. CSP offers 75,000-point welcome bonus ($5K/3mo); Strata Premier offers 60,000 points ($4K/3mo). Key differences: the Strata Premier earns 3x at supermarkets — which CSP covers only for online groceries — while the Sapphire Preferred transfers to Hyatt and offers stronger travel protections. Your spending mix and travel partner preferences determine the winner.

Everyday Spending Earn RatesWinner: Citi Strata Premier
Chase Sapphire Preferred

CSP earns 3x on dining and 2x on travel — strong for dedicated travelers, but only 1x on in-store groceries (gas now earns 3x). At $400/month on groceries and $150/month on gas, CSP earns $102 in those categories. For households with significant grocery and gas budgets, CSP's earn rates leave real money on the table.

Citi Strata Premier

Strata Premier earns 3x on five everyday categories — restaurants, supermarkets, gas/EV, air travel, and hotels direct. At $400/month on groceries and $150/month on gas, Strata Premier earns $198 in those two categories alone. For everyday household spending, Strata Premier is significantly more efficient than CSP, turning non-travel spending into meaningful ThankYou point accumulation.

Annual Credit / Net FeeWinner: Citi Strata Premier
Chase Sapphire Preferred — $100 hotel credit

CSP includes up to $100 each account anniversary year for hotel stays booked through Chase Travel — enough to offset the $95 fee if you book one stay a year through the portal.

Citi Strata Premier — $100 hotel benefit

Once per calendar year, book a single hotel stay of $500+ through CitiTravel and get $100 off instantly. This benefit alone exceeds the $95 annual fee — making the Strata Premier effectively free for anyone booking one hotel stay per year. You also earn 10x on the remaining balance, adding another $40+ in point value on the booking.

Transfer Partners — Hyatt vs. AAdvantageWinner: Depends on Your Loyalty Programs
Chase Sapphire Preferred — Hyatt exclusive

World of Hyatt is Chase's most valuable transfer partner and is exclusive to Chase — Citi does not offer Hyatt transfers. Hyatt points are worth 1.7–2.5¢ each for category 1–4 properties, meaning 75,000 CSP points (56,250 Hyatt points at the 4:3 ratio) can book 3–4 free Hyatt nights at mid-range hotels. If you stay at Hyatt properties regularly, CSP's transfer network has a decisive advantage.

Citi Strata Premier — AAdvantage exclusive

American Airlines AAdvantage is Citi's exclusive partner — Chase, Amex, and Capital One do not offer AAdvantage transfers at 1:1. For American Airlines loyalists, Strata Premier is the only transferable points card that feeds AAdvantage directly. Strata Premier also offers 22 total partners versus CSP's 14, including Flying Blue, Turkish Miles&Smiles, and Singapore KrisFlyer.

Travel ProtectionsWinner: Chase Sapphire Preferred
Chase Sapphire Preferred — Primary rental car + full suite

CSP includes primary rental car insurance (covers damage without filing with your personal auto insurance first), trip cancellation/interruption up to $10,000/trip, trip delay reimbursement after 12 hours, baggage delay insurance, and travel accident insurance. Primary rental car coverage alone is worth $10–$30/rental day versus the Strata Premier's secondary coverage.

Citi Strata Premier — Secondary rental + standard protections

Strata Premier's rental car coverage is secondary — you must first file with your personal insurance before the card kicks in. Trip cancellation and delay protections are included, but the overall travel insurance package is less comprehensive than CSP's, particularly for frequent renters.

The Real Decision: Where Do You Spend?

If most of your non-dining spending is on groceries, gas, and everyday categories: Strata Premier earns dramatically more — potentially $150–$300 more per year on those categories alone. If you travel frequently, rent cars, stay at Hyatt hotels, and want the strongest travel protections: CSP wins on those dimensions. Many cardholders could benefit from holding both — using Strata Premier for everyday spending and CSP for travel bookings and Hyatt transfers.

Who Should Get Each Card

Choose Chase Sapphire Preferred if…

  • You stay at Hyatt hotels — Hyatt is the most valuable transfer partner, exclusive to Chase
  • You rent cars frequently — primary rental car insurance is a significant perk
  • You book most travel through Chase Travel to maximize the 5x portal rate
  • You want the strongest overall travel protection package
  • You're building a Chase Ultimate Rewards ecosystem (Ink cards + Sapphire)

Choose Citi Strata Premier if…

  • You spend significantly on groceries and gas each month ($300+/month combined)
  • You fly American Airlines — AAdvantage transfers are exclusive to Citi
  • You book at least one $500+ hotel stay per year (the $100 benefit covers the fee)
  • You want 3x on air travel and hotels direct without booking through a specific portal
  • You want more transfer partner options (22 vs. 14) including Turkish and Singapore

Bottom Line

At the same $95 annual fee, this comparison comes down entirely to your spending and travel patterns. Strata Premier earns more on everyday household spending (groceries, gas) and is the better card for American Airlines loyalists and those who want the annual hotel benefit to cover the fee automatically. Chase Sapphire Preferred wins for Hyatt loyalists, frequent car renters, and cardholders building a Chase rewards ecosystem. Both are exceptional $95 travel cards — the right answer may be getting one now and the other later, using each for its respective strengths.
