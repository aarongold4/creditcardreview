The Ink Business Unlimited is the simplest Chase business card: 1.5% on every purchase, no categories, no caps, no activation. For businesses with diverse and unpredictable spending that doesn't fit neatly into the Ink Cash's office/telecom categories, the Unlimited earns a competitive flat rate at zero annual fee. It shares the same $750 welcome bonus and 0% APR window as the Ink Cash, and pools points with Sapphire and Ink Preferred cards when you want to unlock transfer partners.

The 1.5% flat rate is straightforward: no quarterly activation, no spending caps, no category tracking. Every dollar you spend on the card earns 1.5 cents toward cash back — whether it's inventory, contractor payments, advertising, meals, or travel.

Many businesses get both cards: use Ink Cash for office supply and telecom spending (5%), use Ink Unlimited for everything else (1.5%). Pooled together with an Ink Preferred, all points become transferable Ultimate Rewards.

March 2026: Chase Ended External Bank Transfers of Cash Back

Effective March 27, 2026, Chase no longer allows transferring cash-back rewards directly to an external bank account. You can still redeem as: a cash deposit to an eligible Chase bank account, a statement credit, or toward travel through Chase Travel. This change affects how you access your cash back but doesn't reduce the earned value.

Pairing for Maximum Value

The Ink Business Unlimited earns cash back (Ultimate Rewards points at 1¢/pt) by itself. But paired with the Ink Business Preferred ($95/yr), Chase Sapphire Preferred ($95/yr), or Chase Sapphire Reserve ($795/yr), you can pool points and access Chase's 14 transfer partners at rates of 1.25–2¢/pt. The combination of Ink Cash + Ink Unlimited + Ink Preferred is a classic "Chase trifecta" business setup that maximizes earning across all spending categories.

Bottom Line

The Ink Business Unlimited is the right card for businesses that want a simple, flat-rate no-fee option or that spend across too many categories to benefit from the Ink Cash's specific 5% tiers. The $750 welcome bonus and 0% APR window are excellent for a no-fee card. Pair it with the Ink Cash and a Sapphire card to build the full Chase business rewards ecosystem. For pure flat-rate value, be aware the Wells Fargo Signify Business Cash earns 2% flat with no annual fee — higher than the Unlimited's 1.5%.
