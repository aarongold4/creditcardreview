The Chase Sapphire Reserve for Business launched June 2025 as Chase's flagship premium business card. At $795/year, it's positioned against the Amex Business Platinum ($695/yr) and offers a compelling package for business owners who travel frequently and spend on advertising. The $300 travel credit, $500 in semi-annual Edit hotel credits, Priority Pass + Chase Sapphire Lounge access with two guests, and credits for Google Workspace, ZipRecruiter, and Lyft make the card's effective annual fee substantially lower than the sticker price — for cardholders who use all the credits.

If fully used, credits total $1,500–$1,700+ against a $795 annual fee. Even using just the $300 travel credit + $400 ZipRecruiter + $200 Google Workspace reduces the effective fee to negative $105 for businesses that spend on recruiting and Google tools.

Lounge Access — Chase Sapphire Lounges + Priority Pass

Primary cardholders get complimentary access to the Chase Sapphire Lounge by The Club network (currently 7 U.S. locations, with more opening) plus Priority Pass Select membership for 1,300+ airport lounges worldwide. Primary cardholders can bring up to two guests at no charge — a meaningful advantage over cards that charge per guest or don't allow guests. Employee cardholders do not get lounge access.

$120,000 Spend — Unlock IHG Diamond + Southwest Status

Spend $120,000 in a calendar year and unlock: IHG One Rewards Diamond Elite status (top tier — includes suite upgrades, executive lounge access, and 100% bonus points) and Southwest Airlines A-List status plus a $500 credit for Southwest flights booked through Chase Travel. These are high thresholds, but for businesses routing all expenses through the card, the status benefits add significant value.

Points Boost — Up to 2¢ Per Point on Select Travel

When booking select flights and hotels through Chase Travel, Points Boost can increase point value to up to 2¢/pt (versus 1¢/pt for cash redemptions). This means 200,000 bonus points could be worth up to $4,000 on boosted travel bookings. Specific Partners Boost rates vary by airline and hotel — check Chase Travel at time of booking for current boost availability.

Bottom Line

The Chase Sapphire Reserve for Business is the right card for business owners who travel frequently, spend on advertising and recruiting tools, and can capture the full credit stack. When all credits are used, the effective fee drops well below the $795 sticker — and the lounge access, travel protections, and Points Boost make it a genuine premium travel card for business. For smaller businesses or owners who don't travel, the Ink Business Preferred at $95/year delivers most of the rewards value at a fraction of the cost.
