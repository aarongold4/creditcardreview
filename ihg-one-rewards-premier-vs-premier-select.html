IHG One Rewards Premier vs. Premier Select 2026: Is the New $350 Card Worth It?

The $150 Premier is still the right IHG card for most people. Here is who should pay $200 more for the Premier Select.

📅 Updated November 2, 2026
✍️ By Nathan Blake
⏳ 7 min read
✓ Fact-checked

For most IHG guests, the $150 IHG One Rewards Premier is still the better card. The new $350 IHG One Rewards Premier Select earns its extra $200 only if you will actually use its bigger credits, $300 a year in IHG food and beverage credits and a $200 airline credit, or if you want automatic Platinum Elite status and an easier route to Diamond. Both cards have limited-time welcome offers that end November 18, 2026: 180,000 points on the Premier and 200,000 points on the Premier Select.

IHG Premier vs. Premier Select at a Glance

Chase and IHG rebuilt the IHG card lineup on October 1, 2026. The Premier's fee went to $150 and it picked up new credits, and the Premier Select launched above it. Here are the terms side by side, from Chase's own card pages:

What the Extra $200 Actually Buys

The fee gap is $200. The Premier Select gives back more than that on paper, but only if you use credits that come with conditions.

The math is simple. If you stay at an IHG hotel at least once every quarter and buy one airline ticket a year, the Premier Select's extra credits ($200 more in food and beverage, $100 more in airline credit) more than cover the $200 fee difference before you count the bigger free night or Platinum status. Miss two quarters of hotel stays and the case falls apart, because unused quarterly credits disappear.

Who Should Pay $350

Travelers who stay at IHG hotels every quarter, put dining and travel on the card, and want Platinum or Diamond without the spending. If you check into an IHG property a couple of times a year, keep the $150 Premier.

Earning: Different Categories, Not Just Higher Rates

The Premier Select earns more at IHG (12x from the card vs. 10x) and 6x on dining and all other travel, including rideshare. The Premier earns 5x on a narrower travel list (flights booked with the airline and car rentals) but adds 5x on groceries and gas, where the Premier Select earns only 3x.

That makes the Premier the better everyday card for a household that spends heavily at supermarkets and gas stations, and the Premier Select the better card for frequent travelers who eat out a lot. Note that through December 31, 2026 the Premier also earns 5x on most travel; that broader category ends with the year.

IHG points aren't worth a lot each, so neither card is a great choice for non-IHG spending compared with a flexible card like the Chase Sapphire Preferred. Use the IHG card at IHG and for its bonus categories.

Status: Platinum vs. Gold, and the Road to Diamond

The Premier Select's automatic Platinum Elite is its clearest upgrade. On the Premier, you get Gold and have to spend $15,000 in a calendar year to reach Platinum for the rest of that year and the next. Diamond, IHG's top tier, comes at $25,000 of annual spending on the Premier Select versus $40,000 on the Premier. If Diamond matters to you and you put a lot of spending on your hotel card, that $15,000 difference is significant.

The Premier Select also gives 20 elite night credits a year versus 15, and both cards add 2 more for every $5,000 you spend.

Can You Have Both? Application Order Matters

Chase's terms make the order important:

  • The Premier is not available if you have any personal IHG card or have earned a new-cardmember bonus on one in the past 24 months.
  • The Premier Select only restricts current Premier Select cardmembers and people who earned a Premier Select bonus in the past 24 months.

So you can open the Premier first and add the Premier Select later, but opening the Premier Select first blocks you from the Premier. Both are Chase cards, and the Chase 5/24 rule applies.

If you already hold the Premier, the new $150 fee applies at your account anniversary in 2027, so you have time to decide. Chase hasn't published a formal upgrade path from Premier to Premier Select, so don't count on getting the 200,000-point bonus through a product change.

The Premier remains one of the best mid-tier hotel cards, and the fourth night free on reward stays (on both cards) is the benefit that does the most work. The Premier Select is a good card for a narrower group: regular IHG guests who will use quarterly credits without changing their plans. Whichever you pick, apply by November 18, 2026 to get the higher welcome offer.
