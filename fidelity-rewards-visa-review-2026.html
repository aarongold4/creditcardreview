The Fidelity® Rewards Visa Signature® Card is the best credit card for people who think in decades rather than months. It earns an unlimited 2% cash back on every purchase — with no categories, no caps, and no complexity — and deposits those rewards directly into your Fidelity brokerage, IRA, Roth IRA, HSA, or 529 account. Instead of taking a vacation with your rewards, you're building a retirement fund. Every swipe compounds.

For non-investors who want their 2% as a statement credit or gift card, other options are more flexible. But for Fidelity customers who are focused on long-term wealth building, there is no better card.

How the 2% Cash Back Works

You earn 2 Reward Points per dollar spent on every eligible purchase — no categories, no rotating quarters, no spending caps. The 2% value is only achieved when you redeem points as a deposit into an eligible Fidelity account. Other redemption options (travel, gift cards, merchandise, statement credit) may yield less than 2% value per point.

Critical: The Fidelity Account Requirement

The full 2% cash back only applies when points are deposited into an eligible Fidelity investment account. You need to link a Fidelity account when you set up automatic redemption. If you don't have a Fidelity account, you'll earn less than 2% on alternative redemptions — making the Citi Double Cash or Wells Fargo Active Cash better alternatives. If you do have Fidelity, you already know this card makes sense.

You can split rewards across multiple accounts — for example, 50% to your Roth IRA and 50% to your child's 529 plan. You can also redirect rewards to a family member's account, letting a grandparent fund a grandchild's college account with everyday credit card spending.

The Compound Interest Argument

A household spending $2,500/month earns $50/month (2% × $2,500) in cash back — $600/year. As a statement credit, that's $600. Invested in a Fidelity S&P 500 index fund at a historical 8% annual return:

As Bankrate editor John Puterbaugh noted: $500/year in cash back deposits, growing at 8% annually, could be worth nearly $25,000 over 20 years.

Card Benefits

As a Visa Signature card, the Fidelity Rewards comes with a strong benefit package for a no-fee card:

  • Global Entry / TSA PreCheck credit: Up to $100 in Reward Points when you pay the application fee with this card — a rare benefit for a no-annual-fee card
  • Auto Rental Collision Damage Waiver: Secondary coverage when you decline the rental company's CDW
  • Visa Signature Concierge: 24/7 assistance for travel, dining, and entertainment reservations
  • Travel and Emergency Assistance: Services and referrals during emergencies abroad
  • Lost Luggage Reimbursement: Coverage for lost or damaged luggage when charged to the card
  • Zero Fraud Liability: Full protection against unauthorized charges
  • No foreign transaction fees: Use the card abroad with no surcharge
  • APR of 18.74% variable (flat): The same rate for all cardholders — no tiered pricing. Lower than average for a rewards card.

The Fidelity Card's Unique Edge: No Foreign Transaction Fee

The Citi Double Cash and Wells Fargo Active Cash both charge 3% on international purchases — effectively reducing their 2% cash back to -1% abroad. The Fidelity Rewards card charges no foreign transaction fee, making it the best 2% cash back card for international travelers by a wide margin. This is rarely highlighted and often surprises cardholders.

Who Should Get This Card?

Get it if you:

  • Already have a Fidelity brokerage, IRA, or 529 account and want your spending to automatically fund your investments
  • Travel internationally and want 2% cash back with no foreign transaction fee
  • Think long-term and prefer compound growth over immediate cash
  • Want to fund a child's or grandchild's 529 plan with everyday spending

Skip it if you:

  • Don't have a Fidelity account and don't want one — other 2% cards offer more redemption flexibility
  • Want a welcome bonus — the Citi Double Cash offers $200 after $500 spend
  • Have less than excellent credit — the 720+ requirement is stricter than comparable cards

The Bottom Line

The Fidelity Rewards Visa is the most intelligent everyday card for long-term investors. It turns the mundane act of buying groceries and gas into a systematic investment contribution. The 2% rate ties the best available for flat-rate no-fee cards, the lack of a foreign transaction fee gives it a meaningful advantage over Citi Double Cash and Wells Fargo Active Cash for travelers, and the auto-investment feature transforms a modest $600/year reward into $68,000+ over 30 years at historical market returns.

If you're a Fidelity customer who pays your balance in full, this is your card. Full stop.
