Bilt 2027 Changes: How to Keep 4% Bilt Cash (and Whether You Should)

Flexible Bilt Cash is going away for new cardholders. Existing cardholders can keep it, but only by acting before their card anniversary.

📅 Updated November 9, 2026
✍️ By Nathan Blake
⏳ 6 min read
✓ Fact-checked

Bilt is retiring the 4% Bilt Cash option, which it calls Flexible Bilt Cash, as part of its 2027 program changes announced October 27, 2026. Existing cardholders can keep it, but only if they opt out of the new benefits in the Bilt app before their card anniversary; do nothing and you are moved to Housing-Only rewards for good. New cardholders can still choose Flexible Bilt Cash if their card is approved and accepted by December 31, 2026. From January 1, 2027, new cardholders get Housing-Only. Bilt Cash also now expires 12 months after you earn it, effective immediately.

The Two Ways Bilt Rewards Housing Payments

Bilt cards let you pay rent or a mortgage without a fee and earn points on it. How many points depends on which of two reward structures you pick. They are alternatives, not add-ons: you get one or the other.

Flexible Bilt Cash (being retired)

  • Earn 4% back in Bilt Cash on everyday purchases, with no cap, on top of the card's normal points.
  • Housing payments themselves earn no Bilt Cash.
  • Spend $30 of Bilt Cash to unlock 1,000 points on your housing payment, up to 1x the payment. Bilt's example: $60 of Bilt Cash on a $2,000 rent payment unlocks 2,000 points.

Housing-Only (the 2027 default)

Your points on rent or mortgage depend on how much you spend on everything else in the same billing cycle, as a share of your housing payment:

Under Housing-Only, you still earn Bilt Cash, just less of it: the annual card credit plus $50 for every 25,000 points you earn.

How to Keep 4% Bilt Cash

The Deadline Is Your Card Anniversary

If you are an existing cardholder and want Flexible Bilt Cash, you have to opt out of the new benefits before your card anniversary date. If you do nothing, your account moves to Housing-Only at the anniversary and you can't switch back.

The Points Guy describes the in-app path as: Wallet, then your Bilt card, then the Bonus rewards tile, then change rewards structure, then select Flexible Bilt Cash and save. Two things to know before you do it:

  • Opting out is permanent. After you opt out, you can no longer switch between the two structures.
  • You keep the old card credits. Cardholders who opt out stay on the pre-2027 annual credits instead of the new Bilt Cash credits described below.

If you are applying now, note that Housing-Only is the default when you open a card, so you have to actively choose Flexible Bilt Cash. Cards approved and accepted by December 31, 2026 can still pick it; those cardholders also need to opt out before their first anniversary to keep it.

Should You Keep It? A Quick Decision Guide

The choice comes down to how your everyday spending compares with your rent or mortgage.

Everyday spending is 25% to 75% of your housing paymentClose callHousing-Only's 0.5x and 0.75x tiers are about what 4% Bilt Cash unlocks; run your numbers

A worked example using Bilt's own rates: with $2,000 rent and $1,000 of other spending a month, Housing-Only puts you at the 50% tier and 0.75x, or 1,500 points on rent. Flexible Bilt Cash earns $40 of Bilt Cash on that $1,000, enough to unlock about 1,333 points at $30 per 1,000. That's slightly fewer points on rent, but you keep the earnings in Bilt Cash and can choose whether to spend them on housing points. Raise your everyday spending to $2,000 and Housing-Only pays 1.25x, or 2,500 points, which is more than Flexible Bilt Cash can unlock on housing. Run your own numbers, because the answer flips as your spending changes.

Bilt Cash Now Expires After 12 Months

Bilt Cash used to expire at the end of the year, with up to $100 rolling over. It now expires at the end of the month 12 months after you earn it. The change applies to balances you already have: Bilt's example is $100 earned in February 2026 expiring February 28, 2027. Check your balance and plan to spend older Bilt Cash first.

Other Bilt Changes Coming in 2027

  • Palladium ($495): the $200 Bilt Cash and $400 hotel credit become a single $600 Bilt Cash credit each year.
  • Obsidian ($95): the $100 hotel credit becomes $100 of Bilt Cash each year.
  • Credit timing: both annual credits post on your card anniversary, not January 1.
  • Hotel Bilt Cash redemptions: from January 1, 2027, these drop from monthly to twice a year, at $50 per redemption for Blue and Silver members, $100 for Gold and Platinum, and $200 for eligible Palladium cardholders.
  • Point Accelerator: Obsidian and Palladium cardholders on the new benefits can still use it.
  • Daily Rewards: a new rewards program starts January 15, 2027, with terms to come.
  • Hyatt transfers: from January 1, 2027, Bilt points transfer to World of Hyatt at 4:3 (1,000 Bilt points to 750 Hyatt points) instead of 1:1. Transfers at 1:1 remain available through December 31, 2026.
  • Everyday earning rates on the cards don't change.

The Bottom Line

If your everyday card spending is small next to your rent or mortgage, or you value a big Bilt Cash balance for other uses, Flexible Bilt Cash is probably the better deal, and you need to opt out in the app before your card anniversary to keep it. If you put as much everyday spending on the card as you pay in housing, Housing-Only's 1.25x tier wins, so let the change happen. Either way, spend Bilt Cash within 12 months now.
