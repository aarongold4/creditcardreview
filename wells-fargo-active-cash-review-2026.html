The Wells Fargo Active Cash is the cleanest flat-rate cash back card on the market. Unlimited 2% on every purchase from day one — no categories to track, no quarterly activation, no cap on earnings. Pair that with the lowest welcome bonus threshold of any 2% card ($500 spend vs. $1,500 for Citi Double Cash) and $600 in cell phone protection just for paying your bill with the card, and you have an exceptional no-annual-fee option.

The $500 Threshold Advantage

Most flat-rate 2% cards require $1,500–$3,000 in spend to unlock the welcome bonus. The Active Cash requires just $500 — roughly a single grocery run and a couple of restaurant meals. On a $100 bonus, that's a 20% return on your first $500. No competing 2% card asks for less spend to unlock its bonus. The Citi Double Cash requires $1,500 in 6 months for its larger $200 reward — three times the spend.

Cell Phone Protection: Rare at $0 Fee

Pay your monthly cell phone bill with the Active Cash and you're automatically enrolled in coverage for theft or accidental damage — up to $600 per claim, $25 deductible, maximum two claims per year. On a $1,000 iPhone, that's near-full replacement coverage at no annual cost. Most cards with this benefit charge $95+ annual fees. The Active Cash includes it simply for paying a bill you were already going to pay.

Flexible Redemption Options

Cash rewards can be redeemed as a statement credit, direct deposit into a Wells Fargo checking or savings account, at PayPal checkout, applied toward a Wells Fargo mortgage or loan payment, or as gift cards. There's no minimum redemption threshold for statement credits or direct deposit — redeem any amount, any time.

Pair with Wells Fargo Autograph for Transfer Partners

The Active Cash earns non-transferable cash rewards on its own. But if you also hold the Wells Fargo Autograph card (no annual fee, 3x on restaurants/travel/gas/streaming/phone plans), you can pool rewards from both cards and transfer to about 10 airline and hotel partners. The Autograph earns bonus categories; the Active Cash covers everything else at 2%. Together they form a powerful no-fee two-card setup.

3% Foreign Transaction Fee — Not for International Use

The Active Cash charges 3% on purchases processed outside the U.S. On a $500 international hotel stay, that's a $15 surcharge that wipes out the 2% earned. Keep a no-foreign-fee card (Venture, CSP, Fidelity Rewards) for overseas spending and use the Active Cash for domestic purchases only.

Bottom Line

The Wells Fargo Active Cash is the easiest 2% cash back card to own and the best starting point for anyone who wants maximum cash back without thinking about it. The $500 bonus threshold is uniquely low, the cell phone protection is genuinely valuable, and the 0% APR on both purchases and balance transfers adds flexibility. Its only real weakness is international use — keep a no-fee travel card for overseas trips and the Active Cash handles everything else flawlessly.
