The Ink Business Preferred and Ink Business Premier are Chase's two premium Ink cards, but they serve very different businesses. Ink Preferred ($95/yr) earns transferable Ultimate Rewards points with 3x on the most common business categories — travel, advertising, shipping, and telecom. Ink Premier ($195/yr) earns pure cash back with a higher flat rate, including 2.5% on any single purchase of $5,000 or more. The choice comes down to whether you want travel rewards or cash back, and whether you make large individual transactions.

Transfer Partners & Points ValueWinner: Ink Preferred
Ink Preferred — 14 transfer partners at 1:1

100,000 Ultimate Rewards points can be transferred to Hyatt, United, Southwest, British Airways, Singapore Airlines, and 10 other airline/hotel programs at 1:1. Used wisely — e.g., Hyatt peak night awards or transatlantic business class via Flying Blue — 100,000 points can be worth $2,000–$3,000+ in travel. This is the Preferred's decisive advantage for business owners who travel.

Ink Premier — Cash back only

Ink Premier earns cash back that can only be redeemed as statement credit, bank deposit, or through Chase Travel at face value. There are no transfer partners, no point pooling with Sapphire or other Ink cards. The $1,000 welcome bonus is worth exactly $1,000 — no multiplier possible.

Large Transaction Earn RateWinner: Ink Premier
Ink Preferred — 3x in category / 1x outside

For a $10,000 equipment purchase outside a bonus category, Ink Preferred earns 1x = 10,000 points (~$100 value). If the purchase falls within a 3x category, that's 30,000 points (~$375 value). Either way, the Preferred's earn rate on large non-category purchases is meaningfully lower than the Premier's 2.5%.

Ink Premier — 2.5% on $5K+ per transaction

Any single transaction of $5,000 or more earns 2.5% — a rate uniquely available on the Premier. A $10,000 equipment invoice = $250 cash back. A $50,000 vendor payment = $1,250 cash back. For businesses that regularly write large checks, this premium rate delivers real value that the Preferred can't match outside its bonus categories.

Annual Fee ValueWinner: Ink Preferred
Ink Preferred — $95

The $95 annual fee is offset by the 100,000-point welcome bonus alone in year one. In subsequent years, the 3x on advertising and telecom — categories most businesses spend in continuously — generates enough incremental value over the Premier's 2% to justify the $100 fee savings. For most small businesses, Preferred delivers more total value at $95 than Premier at $195.

Ink Premier — $195

At $195, the Premier's fee is $100/year higher than Preferred. To justify this premium on fee alone (ignoring welcome bonuses), you need the 2.5% large-transaction rate to generate more than $100 more per year than Preferred would earn on the same spend. At 2.5% vs. Preferred's 1%, you need $10,000+ per year in single $5K+ transactions for the fee premium to break even.

Ink Premier Cannot Pool Points With Other Chase Cards

This is the most important practical difference. Ink Preferred points pool with Chase Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Preferred, Ink Cash, and Ink Unlimited — you can transfer all your combined UR points to airline/hotel partners via one card. Ink Premier cash back is completely siloed — it cannot be moved to any other Chase account or card. If you have a Sapphire card and want to build a unified UR balance, Ink Preferred is the only choice.

Who Should Get Each Card

Choose Ink Business Preferred if…

  • You want transferable Ultimate Rewards points for travel redemptions
  • You already hold a Sapphire card and want to pool points across cards
  • Your business spends heavily on advertising, telecom, shipping, or travel
  • You want the 100,000-point welcome bonus ($1,500–$2,500+ in travel value)
  • Your individual transactions are typically under $5,000 each

Choose Ink Business Premier if…

  • Your business routinely makes single purchases of $5,000 or more (equipment, inventory, large vendor payments)
  • You prefer straightforward cash back over travel points complexity
  • You spend heavily outside Ink Preferred's specific 3x categories
  • You have no Sapphire card and no plans to pool Ultimate Rewards
  • High annual spend makes the 2.5% rate on large transactions worth the $195 fee

Bottom Line

For most small businesses, the Ink Business Preferred wins. The 100,000-point welcome bonus, 3x on common business categories, 14 transfer partners, and $95 annual fee make it the better overall value for businesses that travel or redeem points for flights and hotels. The Ink Business Premier wins for businesses that routinely make large individual transactions ($5,000+), prefer pure cash back over points, and have no interest in building a unified Ultimate Rewards balance. The $100 annual fee premium is only justified if those large transactions generate meaningful incremental cash back versus Preferred's rates.
