📅 Updated June 15, 2026 — Card Refresh

Chase refreshed the Sapphire Preferred effective June 15, 2026. Key changes for new applicants: welcome bonus raised to 75,000 pts after $5,000/3 months; new 3x earn rates on gas/EV charging and vacation rentals (Airbnb, Vrbo); hotel credit doubled to $100/yr; new $120 Global Entry/TSA PreCheck credit every 4 years; emergency evacuation coverage added. Downside: Hyatt transfer ratio reduced to 4:3 (from 1:1) and 10% anniversary bonus discontinued. All changes listed on this page reflect the refreshed card terms.

The Chase Sapphire Preferred has been the gold standard for mid-tier travel credit cards since its launch in 2009 — and in 2026 it still holds that title. Its combination of a generous sign-up bonus, 14 flexible transfer partners, strong dining rewards, and solid travel protections for a $95 annual fee is simply hard to beat at this price point.

Sign-Up Bonus: 75,000 Points

New cardholders earn 75,000 bonus points after spending $5,000 on purchases in the first 3 months. That's a significant spend requirement — $1,667 per month — so it's worth timing your application around a period of naturally higher spending.

Best Transfer Partner: World of Hyatt

Category 1–4 Hyatt properties cost 5,000–15,000 points per night. With 75,000 Chase points transferred to Hyatt, you can book 5–15 free nights at mid-range hotels worldwide — easily worth 2–3 cents per point in value.

Benefits & Perks

$100 Annual Hotel Credit

Each account anniversary year, you receive up to $100 in statement credits for hotel stays booked through Chase Travel. This reduces the effective annual fee from $95 to $45 for anyone who books at least one hotel stay through Chase per year.

10% Anniversary Points Bonus

On your account anniversary each year, Chase automatically adds bonus points equal to 10% of your total purchases from the prior year. Spend $20,000 and receive 2,000 bonus points automatically — no action required.

Primary Rental Car Insurance

Pay for your rental car with the Sapphire Preferred and decline the rental company's CDW. You're covered for theft and collision damage — primary coverage that pays before your personal auto insurance, saving $15–$30 per day.

Trip Delay & Cancellation Insurance

If your trip is delayed more than 12 hours or requires an overnight stay, you're reimbursed up to $500 per ticket for meals and lodging. Trip cancellation/interruption covers up to $10,000 per person and $20,000 per trip.

Complimentary DashPass (through Dec 2027)

Free DashPass membership — $0 delivery fees and reduced service fees on eligible DoorDash orders. Activate the benefit to claim it. Worth approximately $120/year.

The Bottom Line

The Chase Sapphire Preferred remains the best mid-tier travel credit card in 2026. Its 75,000-point sign-up bonus is worth $937 at minimum and $1,500+ with the right transfer partners. The $95 annual fee is more than covered by the $100 hotel credit and DashPass benefit alone. And the 14 transfer partners — especially World of Hyatt — give you the potential to extract outsized value that no flat-rate cash back card can match.

If you're a traveler who dines out regularly and wants to build a points balance that funds real trips, this is still the first card we recommend.
